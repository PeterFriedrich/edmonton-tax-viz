Data-quality reports

Findings about Edmonton's published open data, written to be checked rather than taken on trust. Each page below downloads its sources live, recomputes every figure in the run you are reading, and asserts a list of invariants at the end — so a report that has gone out of date says so instead of looking fine. None of them depend on this project's own pipeline.

  • The current assessment roll is published under the wrong coverage year
    Property Assessment Data (Current Calendar Year) — q7d6-ambg

    The dataset's Period of Coverage metadata reads 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31, but the rows are the 2026 roll. Proved twice over, once using only the City's own datasets and once against Edmonton's filings with the Province. The data is sound; only the label is wrong.

  • Whole buildings are missing from the 2024 slice of the Historical roll
    Property Assessment Data (Historical) — qi6a-xuwt

    2,448 tax accounts that exist in the 2023 slice and in the current roll are absent from 2024, across 188 neighbourhoods. They are not scattered: 29 addresses lose every account they had. The dataset's own self-audit and an independent control disagree by a factor of 464.

  • What public data can and cannot say about tax-exempt property
    Multiple sources, including Alberta's Financial Information Return

    The roll flags three properties as exempt, against a filed taxable base roughly $15B below it. This report sizes that uncertainty and then shows why it cannot be resolved from public data — including by constructing two entirely disjoint sets of properties that each account for the same gap exactly.

  • Edmonton's open data covers two school authorities, not all of them
    EPSB School Locations (996c-239n) · Edmonton Catholic Schools (gfxq-u8uu)

    The portal publishes point locations for the two public school boards and for no other operator, so private, charter and francophone schools are absent and any distance-to-nearest-school computed from open data is systematically too large. Because this is a claim about absence, the report runs the searches that would disprove it rather than the ones that would support it.
