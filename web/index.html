Edmonton: Tax Revenue per Acre

Height and colour show municipal property-tax revenue divided by neighbourhood area, applying Edmonton's class-differential mill rates. Tall, bright districts generate the most city revenue per unit of land. Height is linear; colour is square-root–scaled to reveal the low end. Grey = set-aside natural/undeveloped land, excluded from the scale.

Important caveats

Revenue is modelled, not billed. It applies Edmonton's class-differential mill rates to assessed value — what the land generates at those rates, not what any owner was charged.

The service cost, stormwater and water/sewer layers are modelled too: estimates from published rates, lot characteristics and zoning — not utility records. They cover only the services this project can measure, never the full cost of running the city.
