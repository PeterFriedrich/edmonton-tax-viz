Edmonton: Tax Revenue per Acre

Height and colour show municipal property-tax revenue divided by neighbourhood area, applying Edmonton's class-differential mill rates. Tall, bright districts generate the most city revenue per unit of land. Height is linear; colour is square-root–scaled to reveal the low end. Grey = set-aside natural/undeveloped land, excluded from the scale.
