Cumulative Impacts," Sustainable Development, March 22, 2016** (routed to
Executive Committee), plus **Attachment 1** (the full 20-page analysis with the
tables). Archived copies:
- 4-page report: https://doniveson.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/IIMP.pdf

modelling/analysis report, not an approved financial commitment. Figures are in
**2016 dollars** and are **projections at build-out**, not realized outcomes.

### The 50-year shortfall
Attachment 1 states verbatim: *"The projected cumulative shortfall over the 50
year analysis period for the build-out of the Urban Growth Areas is anticipated
to be **in the order of $1.4 billion**."* Revenue over 50 years is expected to be
lower than combined **capital + operating + lifecycle** costs.

> ⚠️ **Two distinct ~$1.4B figures — do not conflate.** The City/Province
> **capital** ($1.362B) and the 50-year cumulative **shortfall** (~$1.4B) are
> numerically similar by coincidence but are different quantities. The shortfall
> is the revenue-minus-lifecycle-cost gap; the $1.362B is upfront public capital.
> Any annotation must keep them separate and never present one as the other.

## Currency / supersession

- The figures are **2016-vintage, in 2016 dollars**. Date-stamp them as such.
- The City has since moved to a **Capital Investment Outlook (CIO)** + **Operating
  Impact Outlook (OIO)** framework for the **2027–2030** budget (heard by Council
  Jan 27 & Mar 17, 2026) — the closest current successor to IIMP-style
  operating-impact-of-capital accounting, and the reason this annotation is a
  useful credibility anchor. **However, the CIO is a citywide 10-year capital
  outlook ($10.8B), not a per-growth-area fiscal pro forma** — no 2026 document
  re-presents Decoteau / Horse Hill / Riverview at this fidelity. So the 2016
  IIMP remains the **only** primary source for these three areas' fiscal figures.

## Framing (neutral-tone rule)

State the IIMP's own projected figures + horizon, attribute to CR_2705 (2016),
date-stamp as 2016$ projections at build-out. The report itself lists the City's
options to manage the shortfall (greater density; grow industrial/commercial;
increased residential contributions / MGA review; or reduced service levels) —
present descriptively, no verdict language. This is a multi-decade capital pro
forma, a different unit of analysis from every recurring-cost lens; keep it
separate.
