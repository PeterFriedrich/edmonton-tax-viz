## 3. Technical Learnings

**⚠️ A FIX THAT REMOVES A SURFACE MUST ACCOUNT FOR EVERYTHING THAT SURFACE
CARRIED, not just the row you were looking at when you removed it.** S82 removed
`.tip` on touch to stop it overlapping the card, checked that the card still
showed its headline, and wrote *"the card carries the same `primaryRow` line, so
nothing is lost"* into `DECISIONS.md`, `SPEC_temporal.md` **and**
`MOBILE_USABILITY.md`. It was false the moment it was written — `.tip` was the
only multi-row readout on the device. **All three copies are now corrected in
place rather than deleted**, because the wrong claim is the useful part.

**⚠️ "THE POP UP" MEANT THE READOUT, NOT THE CARD — and the app's own vocabulary
was the tell.** `#hoodmode-btn` reads *"Readout: popup"* / *"Readout: panel"*, so
"the pop up doesn't appear. Panel does seem to work" is a contrast between the
two **modes**, not a report that the card was dead. **It was still worth one
question** — emulation showed the card working, but emulation has lied about
touch twice in two sessions, so "do you see a thin one-line card?" was the cheap
way to separate *card broken on device* from *readout too thin*. He saw the card.

**⚠️ "PARCEL-LEVEL" IS NOT THE SAME TEST AS "NEEDS PARCEL GEOMETRY", and
`PARCEL_LEVEL_OPPORTUNITIES.md`'s framing can park work that is not blocked.**
"Top 3 revenue by zone" looked gated on the ~$300 AltaLIS polygon blocker. It is
**not**: `q7d6-ambg` (value, class) joins `dkk9-cj3x` (`zoning`, `lot_size`) on
`account_number`, both carry a neighbourhood field, and **both raw CSVs are
already in `data/raw/`** (79 MB / 86 MB). The blocker is *polygons*; per-property
**attributes aggregated to a neighbourhood** are available today, which is
exactly what the Glass view's lot-acre metric already does. Warning written into
that doc so the next reader applies the right test.

**⚠️ SPLIT THE GESTURE FROM THE CONTENT WHEN VERIFYING — 7 minutes vs 40
seconds.** `verify-peek.js` is slow because a real tap needs warm-up + ring-stable
targets + an empty-pixel scan. For *what does the card SAY in each lens*, that
buys nothing: switch view, call `openPeek(f.properties)` directly, read
`#peek-read`, screenshot. **This is NOT a licence to test gestures that way** —
S81's finding stands that calling the handler directly proves nothing about the
gesture, and `verify-peek.js` is still the only script driving a real pointer at
the map. Direct call for **rendering**, real pointer for **behaviour**.

**⚠️ THE ONE FAIL WAS THE TEST, NOT THE APP — again.** `card body == the lens
readout` failed on `<br/>` vs `<br>`: `viewTooltip` emits the XHTML form and
`innerHTML` returns the browser's serialisation, so a raw `===` compared **markup
dialect**, not content. Normalised both sides with
`h.replace(/<br\s*\/?>/gi, '<br>')`. Consistent with S80's lesson: a red verify
script is evidence about the TEST as often as about the app.

**Data facts confirmed by inspection (not assumed):** the shipped
`web/data/neighbourhood_value_per_acre.geojson` (406 features) carries **only
per-acre rates** — no revenue total, no acreage, no revenue-by-zone. So **% of
city revenue needs a new column**; it cannot be derived in the browser, and
⚠️ **computing area in JS from lat/lon geometry is the exact silent-correctness
trap `CLAUDE.md`'s CRS rule exists to prevent.**

**Public build has only TWO visible views** (measured, not read):
`money:VISIBLE development:VISIBLE services:hidden ratio:hidden uses:hidden`.
Full-only controls are **in the DOM but hidden**, so Playwright's `click()` hangs
on them rather than failing fast (known trap, cost one cycle).

**Key locations — SYMBOLS, NOT LINE NUMBERS** (use `docs/CODEMAP.md`):
`web/index.html`: `viewTooltip(info, withName = true)`, `openPeek`, `primaryRow`,
`isRevenue`, `REV_CUTS`, `fmtFar`, `FULL_BUILD`, `tooltipFor`, `noHover`.
`web/styles.css`: `#peek-read`, `#peek-read .mixbar`, `.tip .mixbar`.
