## 3. Technical Learnings

### 3a. The published-numbers instrument was wrong three ways, all found before use

The brief's §2 said *falsify the instrument first*. It had **three more defects
than §2 listed**, each of which changed the inventory:

1. ⚠️ **The Money landing view was never captured** — the loop was
   `Object.keys(VIEWS)`, and `VIEWS` has no `money` key. The most-seen surface on
   the site contributed **zero** of the 74 claims.
2. ⚠️ **The public run counted 18 claims a public reader cannot reach** — it drove
   views/services by JS, which works on the public build for controls that build
   *hides*. Same shape as the memory `verify-scripts-bypass-pointer-events`, one
   instrument over. Surfaces now carry `reachable`.
3. `$50k` tokenised as `$50` and merged with the About panel's *"$50 per metre"*.

Corrected: **77 claims on `/full/`, 58 reachable on public.**

### 3b. ⚠️ THE MANIFEST GAP IS STALENESS, NOT EDITS — and the brief had it half wrong

S159's brief said *"nothing recurring can go red if they drift."* **Half wrong,
and the half it gets wrong changes what to build.** Falsified:

- **Four `test_generate_status.py` pins red BY NAME on a hand edit** (total ×10,
  a component sum, the four shares, the rate source). The brief under-credited them.
- ⚠️ **`year` 2025 → 2019 passes 892 tests and every merge-gate guard.**
- ⚠️ **A wrong 2026 mill rate, propagated through `generate_status.py`** — i.e. what
  a refresh actually does — **passes 892 tests and every guard.** Only
  `check_revenue_deltas` (weekly, ≥10% AND ≥$1M) would see a *gross* error; a
  transposition never.

**And the source moved during the audit**: the Open Budget portal republished
FY2026 between 09-05 and 09-15 (501 rows in, 490 out, +$468k). No committed value
touched — but nothing here could have noticed. `DATA.md` §17 now records it.
