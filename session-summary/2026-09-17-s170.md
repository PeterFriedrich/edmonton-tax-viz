  Corrected in §13. ⚠️ **Use `certifi`** (`verify=certifi.where()`) — the box's

- ⚠️ **`www.edmonton.ca` SOFT-404s WITH HTTP 200.** A missing page returns 200
  with a body titled *"Page Not Found"*. My first attempt to locate the 2023
  report reported a dead URL as live. **Check the title, not the status code.**
- ⚠️ **THE RESEARCH REPLY WAS MOSTLY RIGHT AND ONE CLAIM WAS FALSE.** It was
  unusually honest — it self-flagged its unverified numbers, and the number it
  marked *"NOT YET DIRECTLY VERIFIED"* ("7 to 100 years") turned out correct.
  **But it claimed the Matthew Dance blog was *"LIVE (not 404)"* and it is 404**
  over both http and https, while the site root returns 200. Its recommendation
  #4 rested on that. ⚠️ **A source-demanding brief produced a good reply again
  (2nd time), and it still contained a checkable falsehood** —
  `verify-relayed-external-advice` holds in both directions.
- ⚠️ **I rejected one piece of the reply's arithmetic.** It doubled $9.32 →
  **$18.6/centreline-m** reasoning "a two-lane street". Our measured
  lane-multiplicity on the matched population is **×1.80 → $16.8/m**, which the
  brief already named as the best estimate. **Don't trade a measurement for an
  idealization.** Direction is identical either way.
- ⚠️ **THE 404 BLOG STILL HAD THE DOCUMENT.** It had hosted a copy of the City
  PDF, and the Internet Archive has it:
  `https://web.archive.org/web/20240112150443/http://www.matthewdance.ca/s/2020-Infrastructure-Inventory-State-and-Condition.pdf`
