## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Two calls made without Peter (both flagged in the PR body + FINDINGS
  §6):** (1) era bands = median `year_built` per hood (pre-1970 / 1970–89 /
  1990–2009 / 2010+) — a pre-1950 band held only 2 hoods because INFILL
  pulls mature hoods' medians later (median building-STOCK age ≠ plat date);
  (2) ASP hood memberships (`AREAS` dict in the script) are BEST-EFFORT —
  edmonton.ca is Oracle-box-unreachable, so they need laptop re-verification
  against the actual ASP documents before external use. River-valley/ravine
  hoods deliberately excluded as parkland.
- **The modeled utility columns are built-form FUNCTIONS, not billing**:
  water $/dwelling is ~constant across eras BY CONSTRUCTION (per-connection
  model), storm $/acre tracks zoning mix. Era differences in those two
  columns describe built form, not utility behaviour — flagged in FINDINGS
  §2 so nobody over-reads them.
- **Levy is municipal-only** (`data/mill_rates.json`: revenue phase uses the
  Municipal rate; education is provincial, stored for provenance only).
- **`is_set_aside` correctly catches the pre-development anchors**: DECOTEAU,
  RIVERVIEW AREA, RURAL NORTH EAST HORSE HILL all set-aside "Future / Rural /
  Reserve" — the area ledgers include them but flag them inline.
- Reusing `tools/analyze_land_use_diversity.py` as a template saved the data
  wiring — `build_connections(ASSESS, PINFO)` prints its own 1,018-null
  multi-res warning (expected, not new).
