## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ THE OBVIOUS TEST FOR THE T8 PASS WOULD HAVE CLEARED ALL THREE IMPOSTORS
I first ranked the 33 GTFS LRT parents by trip count, expecting garages to be
lightly served. **They are not.** `DL Macdonald Platform` and
`Kathleen Andrews Platform` tie **exactly** with Belvedere and Clareview at
**3,601 trips** — they sit on the alignment and every train is scheduled past
them. What separates them is **structural**: a passenger station has ≥1
`location_type == 2` street entrance; the three have **zero**, all 30 survivors
have ≥1. **Encoded as a rule with the dropped set logged, not a frozen name
list.** ⚠️ The activity-shaped test is the one a reasonable person reaches for
first, and it is wrong here.

### ⚠️ S115'S PROBE GRAPH ROUTED OVER RAILWAYS — AND ITS CONCLUSION SURVIVED BY LUCK OF DIRECTION
`186,931 nodes` is the **all-rows** count for `roads.geojson`, which includes
2,117 railway centrelines. A modelled walk could travel **along the LRT track**
to reach an LRT station. That biases network distance toward euclidean, so the
55%-false-positive finding was **understated**. Filtering to
`centerline_type == "Road"` is also **better connected**: 163,841 nodes at
**99.83%** vs 186,931 at 99.45%. **A probe that becomes the basis of a decision
deserves the same filter review as shipped code.** (`AUDIT_LEDGER` row.)

### ⚠️ TWO CONTROLS, NOT ONE — AND THE MEASUREMENT IS THE ARGUMENT
The bands are **~14× apart**: at 600 m, LRT selects **1.6%** of cells and schools
**21.8%**. ANDing the conventional pair leaves **276 (0.8%)**. A single
"amenity-served" switch would hide that collapse behind one word **and would be a
weighted index by another name** — exactly what `DECISIONS.md` 2026-08-22 refused
for the score. Peter chose two independent toggles + dim-not-hide.

### ⚠️ THE NEGATIVE PHRASING DID NOT SURVIVE A CONJUNCTION
The blurb read *"further than 600 m of an LRT station AND within 800 m of a
school"* — self-contradictory. Rebuilt around **what keeps colour**. Also: the
dim grey is `SET_ASIDE_COLOR`, which **the legend already spends on set-aside
land**, so the blurb must say grey means *"out of band, not zero and not
set-aside"*.

### ⚠️ `applyView` IS NOT CALLED ON LOAD — its own comment says so
My `syncAmenityControls` ran only from `applyView`, so the rows **showed in
Money** on first paint. Fixed by also calling it in the init block beside
`prism-hd`/`prism-row`. **AND it must stay downstream of
`await ensureGridData()`** inside `applyView` — it reads column flags that do not
exist until the grid file lands. Both constraints are in `CONTROLS_MATRIX.md`.

### ⚠️ THE SCHOOL GAP CANNOT BE CLOSED FROM DATA WE HOLD (probed 3 ways)
1. **The roll has no field for what a building IS.** `legal_description` is
   `Plan / Block / Lot`; **0 of 439,685 rows** mention "school".
2. **Zoning does not substitute** — `US` (the zone schools nominally occupy) is
   on **ONE parcel citywide**; `PS`/`PSN` (751/949) are parks. More evidence the
   per-property `zoning` field is not where land-use lives (the 2026-08-01
   polygons-not-field reversal again).
3. **Edmonton's catalogue has only the two boards.** Alberta publishes covering
   lists **as PDFs**.
⚠️ **The open question is NOT "find the data"** — it is whether a hand-transcribed
list is worth going **stale silently** beside two weekly-refreshed feeds.
`ANALYSIS_BACKLOG.md` §13.
