#### B. The liability code is real, mandatory, and names our exact parcels (#206)

Source read in full: ***2025 Recording and Reporting Information for Assessment
Audit and Equalized Assessment Manual*, Ministerial Order No. `MAG:016/25`**,
137 pp, open.alberta.ca CKAN dataset **`1718-1771`**.

⚠️ **It came back STRONGER than the draft dared claim, on three counts:**
- The liability code's **seven** components include a **Tax Code** already
  separating `T` (subject to municipal tax) from `E` (*"assessable but is exempt
  from taxation"*), plus `S` (council-bylaw exempt, still requisitioned) and `G`
  (PILT).
- **No coverage gap:** *"Every assessed property, **including taxable property**,
  must be assigned a tax exemption code"* — taxable takes `NAA`, and the codes
  are *"mandatory in ASSET"*. It is not a field that would be null for most rows.
- **Appendix G names our parcels by statute:** `MGA362(1)(d)` university boards
  of governors (**U of A**), `MGA362(1)(e)` hospital boards receiving Crown
  assistance (**Royal Alexandra, Misericordia, Grey Nuns, Cross Cancer**).

The draft's *"statutory basis is valuable but not necessary"* concession was
**too weak and is retired** — the basis IS the code. Fallback paragraph struck.

⚠️ **THIS CHANGES NOTHING ABOUT THE $125.4M.** The manual establishes the field
exists, is mandatory, and is reported to the province — **not what Edmonton
coded for any given parcel.** Direction of the error remains **unknown**.

#### D. ⚠️ THE FINDING THAT MOVES §0 — the coverage ratio mixes bases

**$800.7M of the $3,855.9M denominator (20.8%) is capital financing**, not
operating spend: debt charges $221.0M, Alley Renewal $174.4M, pay-as-you-go

Valley Line debt $38.4M, plus 9 smaller reserve/arena/local-improvement lines
($70.9M).

So §1's `coverage` divides an **operating** numerator by a one-fifth-**capital**
denominator. ⚠️ **That is the §3 basis-mixing failure sitting in the COVERAGE

## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ How to find an Alberta government document (this worked; searching did not)
`open.alberta.ca` runs **CKAN 2.10.6**. The `/opendata.json` endpoint **404s** —
use the CKAN API:
```bash
curl -sL "https://open.alberta.ca/api/3/action/package_search?q=%22equalized+assessment%22&rows=25"
curl -sL "https://open.alberta.ca/api/3/action/package_show?id=1718-1771"   # resources[] holds every year's PDF
```
⚠️ **Cite the Ministerial Order (`MAG:016/25`), never the resource URL** — the
UUID is a CKAN artifact and the dataset carries every edition back to **2006**.

### ⚠️ `pypdf` WAS INSTALLED INTO `.venv` THIS SESSION AND IS **NOT** IN `requirements.txt`
`pypdf 6.14.2`, installed ad hoc to extract the manual. **A fresh clone will not
have it.** Either add it or re-run `.venv/bin/python -m pip install pypdf` before
re-reading any PDF. Nothing in `src/` or the pipeline imports it.

### Re-fetching this session's inputs (all in `/tmp`, all ephemeral)

curl -sL "https://budget.edmonton.ca/api/operating_budget.csv" -o /tmp/ob.csv   # 7,283 rows, 1,037,656 bytes

and grouping on `department/branch/program`. ⚠️ `account_type` is **`Expenses`
