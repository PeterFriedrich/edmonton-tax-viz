## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ I SHIPPED A DEFECT AND CAUGHT IT THE SAME DAY — read this first

The NRP cross-check derived hood road metres from the **served**
`neighbourhood_value_per_acre.geojson` geometry. That geometry carries a display
setback plus simplification and understates area **~16%** (1.163×), so **every
`$/m` was that much too high**: full reconstruction read **$3,762/m**; it is
**$3,151/m**.

⚠️ **`FINDINGS_utility_validation.md` §4 exists to prevent exactly this**, in
those words, and I walked into it anyway. **Any hood total needs
`load_boundaries()` full-res `area_acres`, never the web geometry.**

⚠️ **The correction nearly stranded.** PR #321 merged mid-session carrying the
**uncorrected** figures; the fix sat on the dead branch until the pre-push hook
blocked it. **That is 13.** Re-branched off master, cherry-picked, confirmed with
`git merge-base --is-ancestor`. **A wrong number on master with the fix stranded
is the worst case of this failure mode, and it happened.**

### ✅ The NRP answers a question we paid research to ask (roads track)

The 2026-09-02 research prompt asked *"is there a better source class — does the
Neighbourhood Renewal Program publish actual per-neighbourhood spend?"* **It does,
and it was already in `data/capital_budget.csv` (committed 2026-08-22).** S134's
round returned those same profiles **only as a trap** (the `CM-25-0000`
undercount) and never as the answer.

Measured: service `Neighbourhoods` = 50 profiles, **$716,465,000**, FY2023–2029;
43 name-match, 45 hoods. ⚠️ **Only 20 profiles carry ≥$8M** and hold $654.3M of
$660.3M — the rest run down to **$1,000** residuals. **The usable series is ~24
neighbourhoods of 406.**

Alley-only profiles are a **separate population** and must be split out first:

| scope | n | approved | aggregate | median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| alley only | 6 | $55.3M | **$521/m** | $568/m |
| full reconstruction | 14 | $599.0M | **$3,151/m** | $3,528/m |

**Against the City's published $1,900/m renew-and-replace that is 1.66×**, 10 of
14 above it, 6 above twice. ⚠️ **The three cheapest are the three that read as
cycle-boundary tails** — the evidence flattering to the current rate is the least
trustworthy. **Not actionable as a rate** (numerator bundles alleys/sidewalks/
lighting/drainage, no published decomposition), but it compounds with the open
25-vs-50 life question: **both push $50/m/yr toward being a floor.**

### ✅ Road-per-dwelling: a good diagnostic and a bad map

Evaluated an externally-drafted task proposing it as the Roads lens's primary
cost driver. **Three of its steps were already built** (`road_m_per_acre`; the
552,113-dwelling `load_water.build_connections` model; the metric itself,
computed 2026-07-07 in `FINDINGS_land_use_diversity.md` §3.2). ⚠️ **Four premises
were wrong**, incl. *"whatever population-by-neighbourhood source the project
already has"* — **there is none**, so the per-capita variant cannot be computed.

Measured on a fresh pull (n=222 residential hoods): median **7.21 m/dwelling**,
p90/p10 **2.8×** vs **1.6×** for the shipped per-acre metric — a better
discriminator, ordering hoods exactly as built form predicts (Garneau 1.09,
Laurier Heights 18.52). ⚠️ **But log r = −0.888 against density**: road supply per
acre is nearly flat citywide (3.1× range, r = −0.10 with density), so the ratio
moves almost entirely with its denominator. **A road-per-dwelling map re-plots
density under a cost-sounding name** — the objection that made the franchise
lenses columns-only. ⚠️ **Four hoods would ship as infinities** — WESTVIEW
VILLAGE is **1,059 dwellings behind 1 metre** of city road (private roads, *not*
the clipping artifact the task predicted).

### ⚠️ THE FRONT-END TRACK — a dependency graph closed two approaches in one run

`CODEMAP.md` gave **nodes**; the question that actually arises ("what breaks if I
move this?") is about **edges**. Added `reference_graph()` to `tools/codemap.py`
— 278 symbols, **852 edges**, in-degree + per-section self-containment.

Two findings, each closing a line of enquiry before Fable pays for it:

1. ⚠️ **`state` is a god object at in-degree 110** (next: `buildLayers` 34,
   `METRICS` 16). Every proposed boundary crosses it.
2. ⚠️ **BANNER-PER-MODULE IS A DEAD END.** Banners mark where someone started
   typing: **`money view (default)` — the DEFAULT lens — has 1 symbol / 29 lines**
   under its banner, while `the citywide budget panel (EXPERIMENTAL)` has **35 /
   1,587**. Self-containment 27–44%.

**Performance mattered here.** The naive one-regex-per-name version took
**3.3 s** against 40 ms for the rest of the tool — unacceptable for a
`PostToolUse` hook firing on every edit. Tokenizing each body once and
intersecting: **78 ms, byte-identical output.**

⚠️ **My first test for it was VACUOUS** — it asserted on `state`'s own edges, but
`reference_graph` strips self-references, so a single-symbol fixture could not
fail whatever the regex did. Rewritten to assert from a *caller's* edges, then
**falsified** by removing the no-dot lookbehind (fails broken, passes fixed).
Same family as S135's *"an assertion placed where the value can't be wrong"*.
