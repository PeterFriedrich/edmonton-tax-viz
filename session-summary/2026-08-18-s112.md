**Every design decision came out of a measurement, and two reversed my first

##### A. Permit COUNT does not work, and a bigger cell does not fix it

| window | ind permits | geocoded | cells | 1-permit cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5yr (2021–25) | 285 | 217 (76%) | 184 | **89%** |
| 3yr (2023–25) | 191 | 132 (69%) | 117 | 87% |
| long (2009–25) | 1,281 | 1,100 (86%) | 835 | **81%** |

Residential 5yr, for scale: 25,149 permits → 4,105 cells.

⚠️ **Geocoding was NOT the blocker** — 76/69/86% tracks residential's 81/69/92%.
I expected that to be the problem and it wasn't.

⚠️ **Enlarging the cell buys almost nothing** (this was Peter's question and the
answer was counter-intuitive):

| cell | 5yr cells | 1-permit | long cells | 1-permit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 m | 184 | 89% | 835 | 81% |
| 400 m | 165 | 78% | 626 | 65% |
| 800 m | 145 | 75% | 424 | 50% |

100 m → 400 m is a **16× area increase that removes 19 of 184 cells**.
Industrial permits are not clustered at the sub-kilometre scale, so merging
cells merges nothing. **The $ spread is near-invariant to cell size** (281× at
100 m, 292× at 400 m, 352× at 800 m) — which is the proof that the
differentiation comes from permit SIZE, not clustering. 100 m kept; it also
preserves the shared cell geometry with the Glass grid.

##### B. What the dollars ARE (Peter's question, and it has a documented answer)

The dataset's own field description: `CONSTRUCTION_VALUE` = **"Estimated value
of construction work."** So:

- a **declared estimate at permit application** — not audited, never reconciled;
- the **permit fee is derived from it** (structural incentive to declare low);
- **land is excluded**;
- **78% of values end in `000`, 26% in `00000`** — round-number estimating, not
  accounting.

So: **not investment.** It is a *scale-of-development proxy*, and the blurb says
"the applicant's ESTIMATED cost at permit time, land excluded".

Same lookup resolved a unit ambiguity: `floor_area` is documented as **square
footage**, so the median industrial permit is 3,843 sq ft ≈ 19 m square — inside
a 100 m cell. ⚠️ **`floor_area` was rejected as the intensity measure: 51%
populated for industrial vs `construction_value`'s 99.6%.**

##### C. The dollars needed deflating, and by more than first measured

| source | 2009→2025 | 2021→2025 |
|---|---|---|
| StatCan BCPI, Edmonton **industrial buildings** | **1.72×** | 1.33× |
| Empirical $/sqft from these permits (warehouses 460) | 1.92× | ~1.10× |

Nominal sums encode construction-cost inflation as development. Deflated to
**constant 2025 dollars**. Long-window industrial is **$3,858.5M real vs
$2,728M nominal** — the 41% that was being read as "less development in the
past".

##### D. $ makes permits disappear — the encoding change is a data-loss change

**13 of 1,314 industrial permits declare exactly $0**, 32 at ≤$1,000, 118 at
≤$10,000. Under a $-driven height those render at zero height and **vanish**,
while the count metric showed them fine.

Fix: cells are selected on the permit COUNT (`ind_n`), not the dollars, plus a
**6 m display floor**.

⚠️ **MY FIRST FLOOR WAS WRONG AND MEASUREMENT CAUGHT IT.** 60 m looked
reasonable and lifted **64% of 5yr cells and 78% of long cells** — including
projects worth up to **$4.6M** — to the same height as $0, erasing the exact
differentiation the dollars were added to provide.

## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ An archived data table is worse than a dead one
Both predecessor StatCan tables return HTTP 200, download normally, and answer
every query — they just stop having recent data. Nothing about using one *feels*
wrong. `--max-time`/status-code reachability checks pass. Only
`getCubeMetadata`'s `archiveStatusEn` says so. **Test that a time series is
still being UPDATED, not just that it responds.**

### ⚠️ Changing an encoding can silently change which records are visible
The switch from count to dollars was framed as a display choice. It was also a
**data-visibility** change: 13 permits that rendered fine as counts render as
nothing as dollars. Ask of any re-encoding: *what does the new encoding map to
zero, and does the old one?*

### ⚠️ A remedy sized by intuition can undo the thing it protects
The 60 m floor was chosen to be "clearly visible" and destroyed 64–78% of the
distribution. **Size a floor/clamp against the distribution it acts on, and
count how many records it touches**, before shipping it.

### ⚠️ "This control ignores its neighbours" has two possible causes
`UI.md`'s #6 heuristic (*if a control ignores the pickers around it, it is
probably a lens, not a detail mode*) named Industrial as its surviving instance.
Industrial ignored the Detail picker because **nobody had built its cells** —
missing data, not a mis-typed control. Reading it as the first cause would have
argued for promoting it to a lens instead of finishing it. Recorded in `UI.md`.

### ⚠️ A decision can lose its stated reason without anyone noticing
`DECISIONS.md` 2026-07-23 tagged Industrial `/full/`-only for exactly one
reason: *it's choropleth-only, so in public it would leave the Detail selector
with dead options*. That is now false. **The tag was NOT changed** — the
question is open at the top of `TODO.md`.
