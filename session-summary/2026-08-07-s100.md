## 3. Technical Learnings

- ⚠️ **THE HEADLINE: every identifier in the assessment datasets churns
  independently, and a check built on any one of them reports churn as loss.**
  Three one-off checks this session, three false positives, one per identifier:
  - **`account_number` — renumbered.** All four hospitals moved into a new
    `114955xx` block. Old numbers gone from the current roll; new numbers absent
    from **all 13 years** of history. An account-based check said the hospitals
    had never existed.
  - **address — re-addressed.** `WESTMOUNT SHOPPING CENTRE NW` no longer exists
    as a street name. An address-based check called **$469.8M "missing"** that
    is not.
  - **`neighbourhood` — renamed.** OLIVER → WÎHKWÊNTÔWIN, 12,237 parcels. A
    per-hood aggregate put OLIVER at **−100%**, top of its own dropout list.
- ⚠️ **Renumbering is ROUTINE, measured**: accounts vanish at **0.15–0.37%/yr**
  in the historical roll, with **2023→2024 spiking to 0.91% / 3,893 accounts**.
  So a vanished account number cannot itself be a finding. This baseline is what
  decided the audit's design; without it the obvious account-diff tool looks fine.
- **Position is stable to under 2 m across a renumber** — Royal Alexandra 1.3m,
  Grey Nuns 1.6m, Misericordia 0.7m, Cross Cancer 0.6m. That is the whole basis
  for the audit. ⚠️ A 0.1 m tolerance reproduces the false positive; a
  falsification test pins this.
- ⚠️ **`legal_description` (plan/block/lot) would be a better key and is
  immutable — it exists ONLY in `qi6a-xuwt`, not `q7d6-ambg`.** Dead end,
  recorded so nobody re-derives it.
- ⚠️ **The two datasets disagree about account numbers for the SAME assessment
  year 2025** (historical `10095840`, current `11495573`). **Cross-dataset
  account joins are unreliable for renumbered parcels.** Bears on the pending
  `qi6a-xuwt` bug report — reasoning suggests renumbering makes its 2,448 a
  **floor** (a renumbered account fails the "in current roll" leg and drops out
  of the gap set), i.e. conservative in the safe direction — ⚠️ **but that is
  REASONED, NOT MEASURED. Verify before citing it.**
- **A guard's threshold needs two dimensions when the population is
  heterogeneous.** Percentage alone fires on small edge hoods (ALCES: +12.7% on
  +$501K, ordinary churn). Dollars alone fire on big hoods drifting 1%. The pair
  fires on exactly one event in all recorded history.
- ⚠️ **Warn-not-fail was right for a reason that only became visible at the end:**
  the event the guard catches turned out to be a **correction**, not a
  corruption. Failing the publish on it would have been exactly backwards.
- **A stale premise does not necessarily invalidate what was built on it.**
  `audit_exempt_institutional.py` *measures* rather than assumes, so it survived
  its own motivating sentence being retracted. ⚠️ **Check before rewriting** —
  the retraction actively invites an unnecessary "fix", which is why both files
  now warn against it.
- **Peter merges within minutes — again, and this time it cost four commits.**
  See §2 In Progress. The memory note on this exists and was still not enough.
