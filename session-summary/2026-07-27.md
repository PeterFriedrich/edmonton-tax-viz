**THE RING ROAD WAS THE HARD PART (all in `build_reference_layers.py`):**
1. **Name-matching alone gives 275.7 km across 590 segments, not the ~75 km
   ring** — every entrance/exit ramp, right-turn cutoff and
   collector-distributor lane is *named* for the highway it serves. Fixed with
   an explicit `road_segment_type_description` allowlist
   (`HENDAY_MAINLINE_TYPES`) → 149 km. **The two `Structure -` types are IN,
   not out** — they carry the mainline over the river and cross streets;
   dropping them gaps the ring at every crossing.
2. **A 2.9 km hole remained in the east leg = 73 screen pixels**, which reads as
   a rendering bug. **Highway 216 runs CONCURRENT with Highway 14** there and
   the feed labels the shared carriageway for Highway 14 only. Confirmed by
   probing the hole: every sample point sits **1–35 m** from a Hwy 14
   centreline. Only `HIGHWAY 14 NORTHBOUND`/`SOUTHBOUND` are the concurrency —
   `EAST`/`WESTBOUND` is Hwy 14 leaving the ring (a 5 km spur if included).
3. Pulling the concurrency in **overshoots** by ~1.7 km + ~1.3 km past the
   junction. Pruned **by topology** (`_prune_spurs`: a ring has no loose ends),
   iterated, **not** by a coordinate cutoff or length threshold.
4. **THE CHECK IS THE POINT:** 149 km looks perfectly plausible for a 75 km ring
   drawn twice, so **no length total would ever have caught the hole**. Only
   "does every arc end meet another arc end" (`_dangling_flags`) did. Note the
   first version of this check tested *per-part closure*, which was right for 2
   arcs and **wrong once the concurrency legitimately fragmented the ring into
   8** — dangling-endpoint counting is the formulation that survives.
5. Both carriageways are kept (~156 km, 28 m apart, coincident at 1 px).

Seven neighbouring municipalities float as names on the backdrop, **on by

**The design decision, and it corrected a locked one.** The Tier-2 rule (feed
names into the existing `visibleLabels()` declutterer, never a second label

switches. **Anything reasoning "labels off ⇒ no text layer" is now wrong.**

  area, and a place is a `Point`. Explicit `prio` sorts first, area second.
  Faking a huge area would have worked and would have drifted.
- `labelZ(p)` indexes hood properties; places carry none → guards on `!p`.
- Box math multiplied by the `LABEL_SIZE` **constant**; with two sizes in one
  pool that over/under-reserves → reads `d.size`.
