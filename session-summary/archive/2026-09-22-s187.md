- **Two surfaces computing the same quotient must share a gate.** The Ratio lens's
  `ratioKept` (`>= d.floor && !is_set_aside`) and the tooltip had diverged. #520
  had fixed the input crumbs but not the surface. Length was never the problem
  for YELLOWHEAD / HERITAGE VALLEY: their stubs clear 1 m.
- The findings doc originally claimed 43 set-aside hoods also printed the row.
  That was **wrong** and was struck in place (`FINDINGS_sliver_floors.md` §2):
  the money view's set-aside handling already hides them. Check that path before
  re-raising it.
- **The boundary-allocation mechanism moves 470× more length than the floor
  removes** (§1). A 50/50 split would move 25 published hoods by more than 5%. That is
  the open decision, not the floor.
- The evidence recheck (`recheck_evidence_notebooks.py`) reports pass/fail **per
  notebook** and keeps no per-claim baseline. Rewording or replacing invariants
  therefore raises no alert.
- Invariants that compare hardcoded constants with each other cannot see a source
  change. Pin transcriptions to the fetched text, and falsify each pin by mutating one digit.
