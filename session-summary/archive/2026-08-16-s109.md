#### B. ⚠️ THE SESSION'S REAL FINDING — one threshold was doing two jobs (#211)

Share (≥25% `rev_frac_inst`) gated both the caveat and the outlined range. Share

⚠️ **AND THE OBVIOUS FIX WAS WRONG.** Ranking by dollar drop fails exactly where
the decision gets made:

| hood | drop $/acre | Δrank | Δcolour |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Spruce Avenue** | **$30,310** (2nd biggest in the city) | **12** | **0.08** |

## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ Two thresholds, and which one a change belongs to
`INST_UNCERTAIN_MIN` = provenance ("part of this rests on land whose tax status
is unpublished") → **words**. `INST_CONSEQUENCE_MIN` = consequence ("the two
worlds do not draw the same thing") → **geometry**. Both surfaces read the
constants themselves, never a copied `0.25`; the verifies assert **set-equality
and the subset property**, not hard-coded hood lists.

### ⚠️ The dataviz validator, and how to use it for a non-categorical colour
`node scripts/validate_palette.js "#hex,#hex" --mode dark --surface "#0a0a0f" --pairs all`
in the skill's base dir. For a single mark against a ramp, run it **once per
(candidate, stop) pair** — feeding the whole ramp in returns the worst pair
*between ramp stops*, which is not the question. Lightness/chroma FAILs are
artifacts of feeding a ramp to a categorical validator; **the pairwise ΔE is the
meaningful output.**

### Driving the camera in a screenshot script
`overlay.setProps({ viewState })` does **NOT** move the camera — MapLibre owns
it. Use `map.jumpTo({ center, zoom, bearing, pitch })` then
`overlay.setProps({ layers: buildLayers() })`. (`map` is the global at
`web/index.html` ~1559.) Zoom 11.6 frames the central cluster; **13.2 at pitch 70
puts the camera inside the prisms.**

### ⚠️ Probing per-view state requires driving the UI, not setting `state.view`
Setting `state.view` directly **bypasses whatever applies `VIEWS[v].opacity`**,
so `state.prismOpacity` keeps the previous view's value and the reading is wrong.
Click the real `#views button[data-view="..."]` and wait. This produced a false
"glass has no translucency" result before it was caught.
