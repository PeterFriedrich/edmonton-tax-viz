## 3. Technical Learnings

### 3a. ⚠️ The whole touch peek card was Times New Roman — the phone's ONLY readout

`#peek` is a `<button class="panel">` whose own rule set `font-family: inherit`.
**An ID beats a class**, so that `inherit` won over `.panel`'s sans stack and
resolved against `html, body` — **which declared no `font-family` at all**. The
card fell back to the browser default.

It matters more than it looks: **on touch `tooltipFor` returns null**, so this
card is the only per-hood readout on a phone. Hood name, readout and CTA were all
serif there. On desktop only the hidden `#peek-go` string was affected, which is
probably why it survived.

**Fix is two lines** — the stack on `body` (so `font-family: inherit`, the idiom
buttons actually need, resolves to it) plus `font-family: inherit` on
`#budget-close`, which was on the UA Arial default.

⚠️ **Measured before AND after**, with a DOM sweep for any element whose computed
`font-family` falls outside the stack. **The pre-fix run is the load-bearing
half** — a clean post-fix result proves nothing unless the probe was shown to see
the bug first (`check-where-the-value-can-be-wrong`, again).

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| desktop 1280 | 2 (`#peek-go`, `#budget-close`) | 0 |
| touch 390, peek + budget open | **4, all VISIBLE** | 0 |

⚠️ **SVG `<text>` was a red herring.** The chart labels set `font-size` but no
`font-family`, which looked like the cause — but inline SVG *does* inherit from
its DOM ancestors, and they measured correctly. **Don't fix the plausible one;
measure which one is actually wrong.**

### 3b. ⚠️ THE SERVICES PANEL IGNORES THE SERVICE PICKER — ten layers, one panel

Driving all ten `SERVICES` keys and diffing the rendered panel returned it
**byte-identical every time**. Select **Fire, Water, Transit or Bike**, click a
hood, and the panel reports **road costs**. `renderServiceCost` reads the fixed
`SVC_COST_BASES`, never `state.service`.

**Peter's chosen direction: "selected layer + its cost twin"** — the panel follows
the picker. ⚠️ **4 of 10 layers (storm, fire, water, transit-supply) have NO cost
twin**, so it must degrade to a shorter form, not an empty group. **Not built.**

### 3c. ⚠️ I PROPOSED A DESIGN PETER HAD ALREADY REJECTED, AND MOCKED IT UP CONFIDENTLY

I offered "cost recovery as a new Ratio denominator, break-even at 1.0" in an
`AskUserQuestion` preview. Peter picked it. **It was wrong twice over, and both
were checkable before I drew it:**

1. ⚠️ **It is not a new layer.** Both cost columns are **exact constant multiples
   of road metres** — `$50.000000/m` lifecycle, `$9.320000/m` operating, **stdev
   < 0.0001 across 400 hoods**. So `revenue ÷ road cost` **is** `revenue ÷ road
   metre ÷ 50`: the existing, public-default denominator rescaled.
2. ⚠️ **`DECISIONS.md` 2026-07-16 locks the framing** — magnitude, **no 1.0
   marking, never "pays its way"** — because the numerator is the whole levy and
   the denominator is one service. Current data strengthens it: **median 10.9×,
   only 2.2% below 1.0×**. A `Per service $` denominator also existed and was
   **retired 2026-09-05**.

Peter's reply was the tell: *"i don't ever remember even asking for break even…
that doesn't mean much."* **He was right and his own July decision agreed with
him.** ⚠️ **Read `DECISIONS.md` BEFORE drawing a mock-up, not after** — a
confident preview makes a rejected design look decided.

### 3d. "Levy" is undefined on every surface, and the noun is inconsistent

Peter: *"that's the big unintuitive addition that makes my eyes glaze."*
Measured: **9 reader-facing `levy` strings**, and the same quantity carries **four
nouns** (Tax Revenue / Revenue / municipal levy / municipal property-tax revenue).

The levy is assessed value × municipal mill rate ÷ 1000 (**Res 7.6254 · Other res
8.3116 · Non-res 24.2229**, 2025), summed per hood, divided by acres. **Education
is excluded** (~2.44 mills residential, ~24% of a bill) because it funds schools,
not roads. Citywide **$2.78B**; median hood **$18,060/acre** ≈ **5.3 average
houses' worth of city tax per acre** — the anchor no surface currently gives.

⚠️ Also found: **`modelled` 17 / `modeled` 17**, dead even, in a project that is
otherwise strictly Canadian (`neighbourhood` 129 / `neighborhood` 0).

All of it is `docs/COPY_DECISIONS.md`, grouped **N/C/J/S/F** so a noun is decided
**once** and applied everywhere — fixing one panel and leaving three others is the
failure the file exists to prevent.

### 3f. ⚠️ Pillow silently rendered illegible captions THREE times

`ImageFont.truetype()` on a missing path falls back to a **~10px bitmap default**.
This box has **three font families and `DejaVuSans.ttf` is not among them** —
only `DejaVuSansMono*` and Cantarell (the memory note
`oracle-box-has-no-web-fonts` says the same thing for CSS; it applies to Pillow
too). Three renders shipped 5px captions before the cause was visible.
`compose-readme-sxs.py` now **raises** instead.

### 3g. Screenshot gotchas

- **Camera is maplibre's, not deck's.** There is no `deckgl` global — it is a
  `MapboxOverlay` (`overlay`) over `map`. Use
  `map.jumpTo({center, zoom, pitch, bearing})`; `deckgl.setProps` throws.
- ⚠️ **Playwright resolves from the SCRIPT's directory, not cwd.** A probe in the
  scratchpad cannot `require('playwright')` — it must live in
  `tools/profiling/` (already in `STACK.md`, and I hit it anyway).
- **Zoom 11.6 pitch 52 is inside downtown.** `zoom 10.05, pitch 47, bearing -17`
  centred on `-113.4870, 53.5180` frames the whole city.
- ⚠️ **Do NOT crop the bottom strip** — the "Beta build — work in progress" badge
  and the MapLibre attribution live there. A tidying crop removes a disclosure
  and an attribution. My first composite sliced both in half.
- The **50 m grid is not prefetched** (2.8 MB). Wait on
  `state.glassCell === 50 && gridData`, not a timeout.
