## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Drive's HTML→Doc import turns `$…($` into an equation** and deletes the text between the two signs. A `$` directly after a non-space character closes a TeX span. - **The operating-budget snow programme was renamed** `OPS/PARS - Snow and Ice Control` from FY2018. An exact match on `Snow and Ice Control` returns $0 for 2025, so match on the stem. FY2026 snow now reads $73.8M (the portal revised it). Parks & Roads growth 2017→2026 is now 1.24× (it was 1.2551).
