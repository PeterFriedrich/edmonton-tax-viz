## 4. Technical Learnings
- **The first lens cannot be corrupted by parcel-level cardinality by construction:**
  roll-summed numerator ÷ geometry-derived denominator are two different bases. The
  WEM/condo bugs only distort per-parcel/per-point metrics (the grid), never the hood
  choropleth. This is the load-bearing insight; the empirics just confirm it.
- **`_point_lot_stats(pts[[latitude,longitude,lot_size]])`** in
  `src/export_value_grid.py` is the reusable deduped-lot-area-per-point helper
  (`SHARE_MAX_M2 = 1000`): repeated lot value <1000 m² counts per unit, ≥1000 m² counts
  once; majority-null multi-unit points → ineligible. Reuse it for the hood lot-acre
  rollup — group eligible `lot_m2` by hood.
- **`scipy` still NOT in `.venv`** — used pearson-of-ranks for Spearman
  (`r.rank_ground.corr(r.rank_lot)`), not `Series.corr(method="spearman")`.
- Real-roll shape: 439,635 accounts, 287,123 distinct points, $237.5B total assessed,
  193,263 boundary acres, 142,161 deduped parcel acres.
- Raw data on disk (`data/raw/`, gitignored): `Property_Assessment_Data__*.csv`,
  `Property_Info__*.csv` (has `lot_size`), `neighbourhoods.geojson`.
