### Method notes that generalise

- ⚠️ **`$?` after a pipe is the LAST command's status.** `guard | tail` then
  `echo $?` printed **0** for a run that actually exited **5** — I nearly recorded a
  falsification as a failure. **Redirect to a file, then read `$?`.** Same family as
  the `check-where-the-value-can-be-wrong` memory.
- ⚠️ **A guard reachable only through a CALLER is invisible to a filename grep.**
- ⚠️ **When a test's first assert is a literal pin, every later assert in it is dead
  code under that mutation.** Falsify the later asserts by mutating something the
  literal pin does *not* cover.
- **Re-measure a backlog item before working it.** Item 4 was false; item 5 was
  understated (one substitution named, whole class actual). **Two of three items in
  this session were wrong as written.**
