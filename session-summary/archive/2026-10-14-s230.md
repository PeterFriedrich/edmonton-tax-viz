## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Overpass 504 is server load, not our query.** overpass-api.de 504'd on a
  one-node query, then recovered ~30 min later. `maps.mail.ru` answered a full
  build, then 504'd four times in a row; `overpass.openstreetmap.fr` answered
  every time and reproduced the file byte for byte. `overpass.kumi.systems`
  and `overpass.private.coffee` timed out from this box; `overpass.osm.ch` is
  Switzerland-only (empty answer).
- **`/api/status` on overpass-api.de returns 406 without a named User-Agent**,
  like the interpreter.
- **The page's theme is `localStorage.theme`** (`light`/`dark`); set it with
  `page.addInitScript` to screenshot a theme.
- **At 1280×800 HOME only 2 counties draw** (Parkland, Sturgeon); the verify's
  "3 of 4" count is at its own taller viewport.
- **Python-driven edits to `web/index.html` skip the CODEMAP hook.** Run
  `.venv/bin/python tools/codemap.py` by hand after them.
- Scratch scripts this session (not kept): `place-variants.js` (font patch
  removed, now uses the shipped face), `light-names.js` (both themes at HOME),
  `rebuild_mirror.py` (runs the build with `HIGHWAY_URL` swapped).
