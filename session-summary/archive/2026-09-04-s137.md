### ⚠️ `docs/DECISIONS.md` has drifted off the contract in its own header

Full measurement: **`docs/FINDINGS_decisions_index_drift.md`**. Headline:

| predicate (from the file's own header) | result |

| **"duplicates no rationale"** | ❌ violated in substance |

Median Decision-cell length by month: **138 → 239 → 557 → 1,629 → 2,219 chars**
(May→Sept). **16× in four months, with no `DECISIONS.md` line reopening the
format.** The Decision column is **88% of the 367 KB file**.

⚠️ **The duplication is PARAPHRASED, not verbatim** — median 8-gram overlap with
each row's pointed-to doc is **4%**, while **98.8% of distinctive facts are
recoverable elsewhere in the repo**. *That is why four months of review never
caught it: there is no wording to match on.* Generalizable lesson — **a
"duplicates nothing" rule enforced by eye, against prose reworded each time, is
not enforced at all.**

⚠️ **It is NOT confined to that file.** `docs/AUDIT_LEDGER.md` opens with *"Rules
(mirror `DECISIONS.md`): … one-line verdict + pointer, never duplicate findings
or rationale here"* and runs **median 1,941 chars/row, max 5,416, 72 KB**. **Fixing
only `DECISIONS.md` treats the instance, not the cause.**

**Remedy is sized:** a trim destroys information in **12 of 247 rows**; ~8 values
exist nowhere else (`0.06%`, `$14,048.73/acre`, `$234,399`, `258px`/`272px`,
`$161.3M`, `$88,038,783/yr`, `152/167/319 ms`) and **must be rescued first**.
