**⚠️ TWO RENDERING INVARIANTS IN THE CHART, BOTH SILENT.** `SPEC_temporal.md` §2.
1. **2024 must LOOK absent.** x is scaled from the **year value**, never the

**⚠️ THE `primaryRow` TRAP — panel mode's reduction is NOT "row 0".** It is right

**⚠️ THE CHANGE-METRIC GATE PARTLY FAILS — measured, `ANALYSIS_BACKLOG.md` §10.**
Peter: *"I've already seen some graphs that have like, a peak in the middle. So
straight average would be 0."* Correct — net change, mean year-over-year change
and OLS slope are the same measure up to a constant.
- **45 of 406 hoods (11%) have a ZERO 2012 share**, so relative change does not

- ⚠️ **Peak-drawdown does NOT fix a peaked series** — Spearman **+0.919** against
  net change, nearly the same ranking. Endpoint vs OLS slope is **+0.959**
  overall, **+0.719** on the humps. **A hump needs a SECOND number** (peak value

- **Two windows are justified**: long vs short rho **+0.734**, sign **flips for
  55 of 406 hoods (14%)**.

  relative-from-first-non-zero-year (puts a 3-year and a 13-year change on one
  ramp).

UI**, and the second only from reading an actual tooltip.

`verify-change.js`'s **first** check and it **recomputes from the raw served
