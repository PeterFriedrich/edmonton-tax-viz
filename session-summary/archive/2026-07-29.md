## S76 — 3. Technical Learnings

**⚠️ THE JANUARY TRAP — the most important thing built this session, and it was
NOT in the plan.** A plain "omit 2024" rule is **insufficient**. The current
roll covers exactly **one** year, so 2025 is publishable *because it is live*,
not because its historical slice is sound (it is short 131 accounts). At the
roll-forward, a naive splice falls back to the defective historical copy and
**re-acquires the defect on a year that renders correctly today — silently.
That is the same shape that let the original defect survive two publication
cycles.** Closed by separating `HISTORICAL_DEFECT_YEARS` (measured: 2024, 2025)
from the omitted set (derived per live year), and then by the archive.

**⚠️ THE ARCHIVE'S THREE RULES — the second one was a bug I shipped and caught.**
1. **Freeze** — only the live year is ever written; a captured year is never
   rewritten (we no longer hold a complete source, so a rewrite is a silent
   downgrade).
2. **Capture everything, use it selectively** — every live year is captured, but
   the archive only **wins** for `HISTORICAL_DEFECT_YEARS`. My first pass had it
   winning everywhere, which **mixes vintages**: the roll carries ~8,200
   post-publication titles, so a clean year served from a capture reads
   measured-later than its neighbours and puts a step in the series that is an
   artifact of sourcing, not of Edmonton.
3. **Automatic** — on ordinary weekly runs, because *a step performed once at a
   date months away is a step that does not happen.*

**⚠️ `refresh.yml`'s COMMIT STEP STAGED `web/data` ONLY.** The archive lives at
`data/temporal_archive.json`, so the capture would have been written and then
**died with the runner — silently, while every log line said success.** Widened
to stage it explicitly. Safe because `deploy.yml` triggers on `web/**` only, so
`data/**` never fires a redundant deploy. **This feature tried to fail silently
in the same shape twice; treat that as its signature.**

**⚠️ A PER-HOOD ACCOUNT-DROP GUARD WAS MEASURED AND REJECTED.** It looked
obvious. But clean years routinely show hoods dropping **20–97%** through
renames, annexation and parcel consolidation (2013 had 9 hoods over 10%), so the
per-hood signal is dominated by legitimate churn and would have cried wolf
weekly. The guard pins **citywide per-year** anchors instead: historical years
tight with a **DROP** as the dangerous direction, **live year deliberately
unpinned** (347 Downtown accounts moved between the 07-06 and 07-28 rolls).

**⚠️ THE OLIVER RENAME.** OLIVER carried ~12,000 accounts through 2024 and
appears as **WÎHKWÊNTÔWIN** from 2025. Keyed naively the series shows one central
neighbourhood dying and another appearing from nothing. **32 historical names
have no current boundary**; 5 resolve by stripping a trailing " AREA", the rest
are left unmatched *on purpose* (GLENRIDDING AREA, MEADOWS AREA, TERWILLEGAR
AREA look like the pattern but have no exact match — never guessed at). The
rename is enforced **inside `build_temporal_table`**, not just in the loaders,
because it is the one invariant that fails silently — a test caught exactly that.

**⚠️ AN UNMATCHED HOOD STAYS IN THE DENOMINATOR.** The metric is share of the
*citywide* base, so an unrenderable hood is still real value. Dropping it from
the base would silently inflate every other hood's share. 24 unmatched hoods =
**0.391%** of value. Consequence: **`web/data/temporal.json`'s shares
deliberately do NOT sum to 1.**

**⚠️ THE WEB ENCODING SCALES WERE MEASURED, NOT CHOSEN.** Verbose floats = 129 kB
(over budget). The obvious **$0.1M value unit is 10 kB smaller and puts the
smallest hood ($57.5k) out by 74%**; $10k is still 7.7% out. **$1k** holds the
worst case to 0.87% and fits at 89.2 kB. Shares in **ppm** (100× finer than the
2-dp-of-a-percent the UI shows).

**⚠️ I NEARLY REPORTED A PERF BUG THAT WAS MY OWN MESS.** The pipeline appeared
to export `temporal.json` twice. It was **two concurrent background runs sharing
`/tmp/run.log`**. A clean single run exports once. Verified before saying
anything.

rebuilt `dev_grid`, `neighbourhood_value_per_acre`, `roads`, `value_grid`,
