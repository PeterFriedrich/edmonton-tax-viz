#### What was built

The Glass view's 100 m grid now marks cells whose levy sits on

##### A. ⚠️ IT WAS NEVER BLOCKED ON DATA — `TODO.md` WAS WRONG FOR FOUR DAYS

`TODO.md` filed this as *"Blocked on data, not on design... the 100 m grid
cells carry no institutional share"* from 2026-08-15. **False.**
`revenue_by_zone` was already assigning every property a zoning category
(`src/revenue_by_zone.py:139`, the `sjoin`) and **throwing it away** after its
neighbourhood `groupby`. The cell share was one regroup off an existing join.

Now extracted as **`property_zone_categories`**, computed **once** in `main.py`
and passed to both consumers. Two reasons it must be one call, not two:
the ~440k point-in-polygon pass costs the same again, **and** a second join
could drift from the first. A test asserts
`revenue_by_zone(a, z) == revenue_by_zone(a, z, categories=cats)` exactly.

##### B. The distribution decided the design, and it is BIMODAL

Measured independently twice (ad-hoc script, then the pipeline — they agreed
to the cell):

| threshold | cells | % of 34,671 | % of city levy |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥10% | 714 | 2.1% | 5.1% |
| ≥25% | **624** | 1.8% | 4.9% |
| ≥50% | 555 | 1.6% | 4.8% |
| ≥99% | **467** | 1.3% | 4.5% |

⚠️ **Of the 714 cells above 10%, 467 are above 99% and only 159 sit between
10% and 50%.** At 100 m a cell is institutional or it is not. So **one
threshold, not the hood level's two** — there is nothing for a consequence
tier to narrow. Moving the threshold across 0.10–0.99 changes the marked set
by **247 of 34,671 cells**; the hood equivalent moved 15 hoods to 6.
`GLASS_INST_MIN = 0.25`, matching `INST_UNCERTAIN_MIN`.

⚠️ **It lands where it matters: 18 of the top 100 cells by $/acre are flagged**,
the tallest at **$5.66M/acre, 100% institutional** (citywide max $12.6M/acre).
Flagged cells are lower on average (median $9,261 vs $18,127) but heavier at
the top (p90 $97,610 vs $53,673) — schools and parks at the bottom, hospitals
and towers at the peak.

##### C. ⚠️ THE FIRST FORM WAS WRONG AND ONLY THE SCREENSHOT CAUGHT IT

I first drew a bare azure **wireframe cage** (`filled: false, wireframe: true`),
following `SPEC_revenue.md`'s *"form carries the shared identity"* rule, because
a cage reads even on a 100%-institutional cell whose levied endpoint is 0.

**deck 9.0.38's `GridCellLayer` ignores `filled: false`, draws its DEFAULT
OPAQUE BLACK fill, and never renders the wireframe at all.** The flagged cells
came out **solid black**.

⚠️ **Every layer prop read back correctly and all 14 assertions passed.**
`filled` reported `false`, `wireframe` reported `true`, `getLineColor` was the
right azure — and the render was black, because the prop that mattered
(`getFillColor`, defaulted to `[0,0,0,255]`) was one I never asserted on.
**`GridCellLayer` is fill-only; an outlined cell needs a different layer class.**

**Shipped form is the hood band's own**: two filled `GridCellLayer`s at the
SAME alpha — `glass-inst-levied` at `height x (1 - inst_frac)` and
`glass-inst-cap` at full height — overlapping from the ground to the levied
height so that section composites denser while the cap stays faint. *This much
regardless, that much depending on an unpublished status.* Both ride
`state.prismOpacity` (0.6); at a fixed 1.0 the azure read as an object sitting
ON the composition rather than part of it.

##### D. The ramp fill SURVIVES here — a knowing divergence, recorded

Money **flattens** a banded hood and lets two azure prisms carry it, because
*"a ramp-coloured floor under the band would assert the very value the band
exists to withhold"*. Doing that to 624 cells would delete them from a view
whose entire content **is** ramp-coloured cells — including the tallest flagged
cell in the city. **Losing the magnitude of a huge uncertain spike is a worse
error than over-asserting it.** Cells keep height + colour and gain the band.
`DECISIONS.md` 2026-08-19 records this as a deliberate divergence.

##### E. ⚠️ MY OWN STATED COLOUR RISK DID NOT SURVIVE MEASUREMENT

I argued in advance that blue cells would collide with **cividis's dark-blue
low end** and read as "low value". Rendered on all three ramps, that is
**false**: `#2ec4ff` is far brighter than cividis's `#00224e`, so a flagged low
cell reads as bright cyan, not as a low-value cell. Also checked **`glow`**,
whose near-white peak (`#fff6e4`) is the case `SPEC_revenue.md` says defeats a
white outline — azure reads as ice-blue against it. ⚠️ The existing ΔE work
validated azure as an **outline against ramp stops**; as a translucent **fill**
it blends with them, so this needed re-checking rather than inheriting.

## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ A "BLOCKED ON DATA" NOTE IS A CLAIM, NOT A FACT
`TODO.md` said the cell-level institutional share needed new data. It needed a
`groupby`. The claim sat unchecked from 2026-08-15 to 2026-08-19 and would have
kept deferring the work indefinitely, because nothing about reading it prompts
you to test it. ⚠️ **This is the SECOND time this file has been wrong about a
blocker** (`todo-can-lag-executed-work`). **Re-measure a stated blocker before
believing it** — the TODO entry is now corrected and says so in place.

### ⚠️ ASSERTIONS CAN ALL PASS WHILE THE PICTURE IS WRONG
The wireframe cage passed 14/14 because I asserted the props I *set*
(`filled`, `wireframe`, `getLineColor`) and not the one the library
*defaulted* (`getFillColor` → opaque black). A layer-prop test verifies what
you told the library; it cannot verify what the library does with it.
**For any render change, LOOK at it** — the screenshot is not decoration, it is
the only check that covers the defaults you never named. (Sibling of S112's
*"a finer rendering audits the aggregate"*.)

### ⚠️ A VALIDATED COLOUR IS VALIDATED FOR ITS ROLE, NOT FOR ITSELF
`#2ec4ff` had brute-force ΔE numbers across all 15 ramp stops — **as an
outline**. Used as a translucent **fill** it composites with the ramp instead
of sitting against it, which the ΔE work never measured. Re-render before
reusing a colour in a new role, even a colour with a paper trail.

### A GHOST WORKFLOW OUTLIVES ITS FILE
A GitHub workflow stays in the Actions UI as long as it has **runs**, even with
no file and no branch. `grep` over the repo finds nothing; `gh workflow list
--all` finds it. Deleting the runs is what removes it.

### ⚠️ ONE SHARE SERVES BOTH DENOMINATORS — deliberately no `_lot` sibling
`inst_frac` has no ground/lot pair, matching `rev_frac_inst`. Which land is
institutional is a fact about the **land**; only a CONSEQUENCE measure
(`instShiftMoney`) has to follow the denominator toggle, and the Glass band has
no consequence tier. Don't "finish the job" by adding `inst_frac_lot`.

### ⚠️ THE ROUNDING IS LOAD-BEARING AT THE TOP END
`inst_frac` ships at 4 decimals. **463 of the 467 near-full cells are exactly
1.0** and collapse their levied base to zero; the other 4 (0.9911–0.9995) keep
a real, invisible sliver. A test asserts BOTH — forcing the near-full ones to
zero would be a lie about the remainder.

### ⚠️ THE BLURB IS BUILT BEFORE THE LAYERS ARE
`setView` writes `#title-p` **before** `buildLayers()` — deliberately, since the
title block is chrome the label sweep measures. My first `glassInstCells()` read
`gridData.cellsFor`, which only the render populates, so the sentence was
silently **absent on entry** and appeared only after some later rebuild. It now
filters `gridData.cells` directly and is order-independent.
