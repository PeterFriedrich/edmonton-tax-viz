## 3. Technical Learnings

### THE METHOD RESULT — asking for raw excerpts beat every round of checking arithmetic

**This is the session's most transferable finding.** Rounds 1–3 were the
researcher checking my arithmetic and internal logic; all of it held up. Round 4
they changed the request: *"paste the actual JSON ... I want to see what's
actually stored, not a description of it"* — explicitly **not** a re-audit.
**That round found four defects the previous three could not have**, because
every one was a mismatch between what I was correctly summarising and what the
files actually said.

⚠️ **All four were SPLIT TREATMENT** — a demotion or caveat landing in one file
and not its neighbour. Which is the failure mode `city_unit_costs.json` records
as *reason (1)* of its own 2026-09-06 re-scope. **The file documented the
disease and then caught it.**

1. `roadway_ops.rescoped_2026_09_06` reason (3) still asserted the **1.29× "the
   two sources do not disagree about roads"** clause. `DECISIONS.md` and
   `DATA.md` §13 both demoted it 2026-09-08; the JSON did not.
2. `_two_bases` stated the **5.4× gap bare**, sitting above the block that says
   not to read it as a fact about a road metre. It is the first thing a reader hits.
3. `roadway_om_renewal` **had no unit caveat at all** — the block holding

4. `DATA.md` §13's first roadway bullet still cited the **3%-of-value rule as a

**Generalisation: periodically re-read the shipped files verbatim instead of
trusting your own summary of them.** Peter asked for this to become a recurring

### THE 1.73 / 1.80 / 2.19 RESOLUTION — one of the three is not the right KIND of quantity

A correction factor here is a **lane-multiplicity**: lane-km ÷ centreline km **on
ONE population**. Measured:

| ratio | construction | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **1.73** | 11,000 ÷ 6,335 (roads **+ alleys**, both sides) | matched, **whole inventory** — alleys are one lane so it is diluted downward. A **LOWER** bound, never interior. |
| **1.80** | 6,593 ÷ 3,666 (collector+local, both sides) | matched **and** the right population. **The best estimate → $16.8/m.** |
| 2.01 | 10,093 ÷ 5,024 (all roads, both sides) | matched; includes arterials, which run 2.58. |
| **2.19** | 11,000 ÷ 5,024 (numerator **incl.** alleys, denominator **excl.**) | **not a lane-multiplicity at all** — mismatched. An **UPPER** bound by construction. |

⚠️ **So $16.8/m is a best estimate and $20.4/m is a bound. Do not average them
or present them as rival measurements.** The repo was already internally
consistent (1.80→$16.8, 2.19→$20.4); 1.73 never entered it. **The defect was
labelling, not arithmetic** — nothing said the endpoints had different standing,
which is what let 1.73 look interior.

⚠️ **A SECOND, INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION OF THE LANE-KM UNIT FALLS OUT OF THIS,
and it does not lean on the alley cross-check.** The 2020 class figures are
roads-only at **10,093 lane-km**; add the feed's **1,311 km of alleys** (one
lane, so lane-km = centreline km) → **11,404**, within **3.7%** of the City's
*"~11,000"*. The centreline reading of the same total is 6,335 km — **42% low**.
Sanity check alongside it: all roads come to **2.01 lanes per centreline km**,
i.e. essentially two, which says the relayed 2020 split is internally sensible
even though its blog is 404.

### ⚠️ `Alley-Commercial` WAS BEING CHARGED AS LOCAL ROAD — found chasing a 1 km rounding gap

Peter flagged that 1,358 + 926 + 2,740 + ~6 ≠ 5,029 and explicitly said **not**
to chase it. Chasing it found a real defect.

**The rounding half is benign** — measured full precision:
`alley-classed 5.7640 · arterial 1357.6948 · collector 926.1970 · local
2739.6499 · nan 0.1057 · TOTAL 5029.4114`. Three of four listed terms round up
(+0.69) while the true total rounds down. **5,029 is right, the components are
right, they cannot be added.**

**The `nan` term was the finding.** It is `Alley-Commercial` — a **16th** class
code. `DATA.md` §6 recorded the enumeration as **closed at 15 values**
(2026-07-01) and it **grew**.

⚠️ **`_classify` FAILS OPEN: `DEFAULT_GROUP = "local"`.** So the unmapped rows
were **counted as local road**, not dropped. *(I stated the opposite in-session
before checking, and corrected it — the direction is the whole point.)* That
contradicts the alleys-out decision that **function governs**, which
`test_functionally_alley_road_rows_excluded` already pins for
`Alley-Residential`. **So this was an existing decision leaking through a code
that postdated the dict — not a new judgement call**, which is why it was fixed
rather than proposed.

**Magnitude: 2 rows, 106 m against 3,666 km of collector+local — 0.003%.** The
served file moves only on the next refresh. **The class of failure is the point.**

⚠️ **The warning had been firing on every pipeline run, into a log nobody reads.**
`_classify` warns loudly on an unmatched code. The guard worked; the channel didn't.

### ⚠️ THE UNIT DEFECT IS A CLASS, NOT AN INCIDENT — and it now has a field

**Peter's own read, and it is the session's most durable outcome:** *"actually
getting messed up by unit issues, which i really didn't think about till now."*

**A rate can be wrong in three ways: PROVENANCE (which publication), VINTAGE
(which year), DENOMINATOR (what the "per" counts).** `city_unit_costs.json` had
dedicated fields, prose and guards for the first two and **nothing at all** for
the third. ⚠️ **The existing `units` key is not that field and never was** — it
describes what *we* compute (*"dollars per road-metre per year"*), never what the
*City's* figure was per. The source denominator lived only inside prose
`derivation` strings, where *"$65,671,000 / ~11,000 km citywide road network"*
read as obviously fine **for a year** while meaning **lane-km** against
centreline metres.

✅ **Added `source_denominator` = `{unit, confidence, why}` to all 7 blocks**
(PR #379). `unit` ∈ {`lane-km`, `centreline-km`, `route-km`,
`not-a-length-rate`, `UNKNOWN`}; `confidence` ∈ {`ESTABLISHED`, `ASSUMED`,
`UNEXAMINED`, `N/A`}.

⚠️ **FILLING IT IN WAS THE FINDING. Only ONE of seven is ESTABLISHED.**

| block | unit | confidence | consumed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `roadway_ops` | **lane-km** | **ESTABLISHED** | yes |
| `roadway_om_renewal` | UNKNOWN | ASSUMED (centreline) | yes |
| `roadway_renewal` | UNKNOWN | ASSUMED — *inherits the above, it IS the $1.9M half* | no |
| **`bikeway_ops`** | **UNKNOWN** | **UNEXAMINED** | ⚠️ **YES** |
| `bikeway_capital` | UNKNOWN | UNEXAMINED | no (inert) |
| `transit_ets` / `fire_response` | not-a-length-rate | N/A | budget, no length |

⚠️ **THE LIVE EXPOSURE IS `bikeway_ops`, AND IT IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL.** It is
consumed (`bike_ops_dollars_per_m`), and **both halves are unexamined**:
(a) **$178/km comes from the same Taproot article, same sentence family, as the
RETIRED $1,285/km** — the article that attaches *"linear kilometres"* to the
~11,000 and is the only source saying linear; (b) the **$20,100/km** snow half
divides ~$30.15M by a ~1,500 km *"cleared network"* whose **membership** was
already flagged (`denominator_mismatch`) but whose **unit** never was.
⚠️ **The distinction bites harder on bikeways than roads** — a bikeway is often
one direction per side of a street, so route-km and lane-km can differ ~2× on
the same street.

⚠️ **`UNKNOWN`/`UNEXAMINED` ARE CORRECT ANSWERS AND THE TESTS PERMIT THEM
DELIBERATELY.** Requiring a resolved unit would get the field filled in with
guesses — which is the exact failure it exists to prevent. **The field's value
is that it forces the question once per rate**, which is the step nobody took.
And ⚠️ **do not resolve bikeway by arithmetic**: the standing rule is that rates
come from a named City publication after a scoped question. The right move is a
bikeway question in the next research round, the same shape as Q1(a).

### Method notes that generalise

- ⚠️ **`sleep N` chained to another command is BLOCKED by the harness.** Use
  `run_in_background: true` with an `until <check>; do sleep 3; done` loop.
- **Reading `data/raw/roads.geojson` through geopandas takes ~100 s** on this
  box. Two such runs happened; both were backgrounded.
- ⚠️ **`git checkout master` FAILS with uncommitted changes** and leaves you on
  the old (merged) branch — the subsequent `gh pr create` then targeted the dead
  branch and errored. **`git stash` first**, then checkout, pull, branch, pop.
- **`load_unit_costs` reads only `value` fields**, so documentation keys can be
  added to `city_unit_costs.json` freely — but `json.loads` the raw text after
  a string edit to confirm it is still valid.
