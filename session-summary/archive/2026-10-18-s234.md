## 3. Technical Learnings
- **ResizeObserver lags under software GL**: a view switch's frames are slow, so
  layout synced by RO was stale at 600 ms and once at 2.5 s. Verify checks on
  RO-driven layout must poll (`waitForFunction`), not sleep.
- "Light-mode F2" was not light-specific — pure layout (views row 390 px vs the
  title column ending at search/? = 462 px).
- `verify-theme.js` takes ~10 min on this box; use `timeout 590`.
- Our total vs the bylaw levy is the cleanest outside check we have; it puts a
  size (11%) on ASSUMPTIONS A1/A2.
