**(b) THE BIKE SLIVER QUESTION IS ANSWERED — AND IT CLOSED S7'S BIKE SURFACE
RATHER THAN GIVING IT A FLOOR.** PR #491. Carried unactioned from S175.

- ⚠️ **S175's framing was WRONG and this is the load-bearing correction.**
  `bike_m_per_acre` = 3.8e-08 is the **MINIMUM** of the distribution, not the
  metric's value. **Median 7.07, max 42.2** — the metric was healthy and only
  the tail was wrong. That is why the fix is a one-line filter and not a
  rebuild. **Reproducing the symptom before acting is what caught this**
  (`todo-can-lag-executed-work`).
- **Cause, confirmed first-hand:** Beacon Heights' entire bike network was
  **ONE overlay piece of 0.000011 m** — 11 micrometres. Where a route runs
  along a boundary the route line and the polygon edge are nominally
  coincident but disagree in the low decimals (separately digitized layers),
  so `gpd.overlay` hands a crumb to the neighbour.
- ⚠️ **The defect was an ASYMMETRY, not a magnitude.** The display path
  already thins slivers (`WEB_MIN_PART_M`, on **welded** geometry), so the map
  **drew no bike line in Beacon Heights while the tooltip reported a number.**
  Display filtered, metric unfiltered.
- **Fix:** `MIN_PIECE_M = 1.0` in `src/load_bike.py`, applied in `load_bike`
  **only** (not `_prepare_segments`, which the display path shares).
  **13.4816 m of 981.4 km (0.00137%), 40 of 5050 pieces, exactly 3
  neighbourhoods zeroed** — BEACON HEIGHTS, AVONMORE, ANTHONY HENDAY BIG LAKE,
  **each named in the log line**. `join_and_calculate.py:531` already defaults
  an absent neighbourhood to a true 0 m and reports the count, so nothing
  drops silently.
- **Why 1 m is not a materiality judgement:** it sits in an **empty gap**.
  Per-neighbourhood totals run 11 µm → 5 cm → 17 cm → **3.39 m**. Nothing
  lives between 0.17 and 3.4, so the cut removes only the physically
  impossible. A route that genuinely clips a corner (King Edward Park, 3.4 m)
  **survives** — calling that too small to count is editorial and belongs in
  `COPY_DECISIONS.md`.

| floor | km dropped | hoods zeroed | hoods changed >1% |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 m** | 0.013 | **3** | 3 |
| 5 m | 0.194 | 5 | 8 |
| 20 m | 4.558 | 6 | **77** |

- ⚠️ **`MIN_PIECE_M` is deliberately NOT `WEB_MIN_PART_M = 20`, and
  de-duplicating them would be a silent 25× widening.** That constant applies
  to **welded** geometry where 20 m means a 20 m *continuous stretch*; per raw
  overlay piece it moves **77** neighbourhoods instead of 3.
  `test_metric_floor_is_not_the_display_constant` pins them apart **because
  merging them looks like a tidy-up.**
- **Falsified two-sided** (the point being that a one-sided mutation would not
  have caught a floor set too high): removing the floor reddens the two drop
  tests while the keep test stays green; raising it to 20 reddens the keep test
  and the constant test while the drop tests stay green. Control green.
