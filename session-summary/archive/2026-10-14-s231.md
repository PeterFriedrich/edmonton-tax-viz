## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Raw `web/` is not what readers see.** Served directly it renders as the
  full build (Uses, Lab, City budget) with no Beta badge. Build with
  `scripts/build_site.py --src <web> --out <dir>` and render `<dir>/index.html`
  (public) or `<dir>/dev-build-full/index.html`. The built public 360×780
  light shot reproduces light-mode F3 (badge over the legend's last line).
- **Render noise floor:** two SwiftShader renders of one tree differ by ≤ 7 px
  at > 40 levels (≤ 31 px at > 8). A first look at a 300 px "diff box"
  between identical trees was anti-aliasing, not animation; the page has none.
- ~15 s per shot (launch + load + 1.5 s settle); 6 shots ≈ 88 s.
- The first-visit guide never opens under automation (`maybeAutoGuide`
  returns on `navigator.webdriver`), so no dismissal step is needed.
- Register statuses lag like `TODO.md` does: 27 of 50 `open` rows were stale.
  The recount script pattern: parse rows between `## Findings register` and
  `## Queued`, 6 cells each.
- `git archive <ref> web | tar -x -C <dir>` is the cheap way to get a base
  tree; no worktree bookkeeping.
