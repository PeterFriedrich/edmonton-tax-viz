## 3. Technical Learnings
- **The ramp-middle compression can't be fixed by a ramp alone.** The four money metrics' bulks sit in different places: Revenue, Residential and Value at t 0.55–0.75, Non-res at 0.17–0.43, and linear mode lower still. A mild warp gives landing 3.2 → 5.6 but Non-res 3.5 → 2.4 and linear 4.7 → 3.1. It is a transform decision; numbers are on the TODO.
- **Capping a reversed ramp's dark end improved the middle.** Inferno 0.25–0.99 gives a middle ΔE of 3.7 vs 3.2 for 0.08–0.92, because the middle crosses more hue.
- **deck.gl attribute buffers are reused and over-allocated.** Values past `layer.getNumInstances()` are stale: the selected-hood prism carried money-view colours into Change and Ratio. Any probe reading `attributes[...].value` must stop at `getNumInstances() * size` (`verify-theme.js` `collect` now does).
- **Measurement scripts are in the scratchpad (not committed):** `.../scratchpad/light/cm.py`, a validated ΔE2000, WCAG contrast and Machado CVD library. It reproduced S186's 14.5% and the ΔE middles exactly. The method is described in `docs/UI.md`; the numbers are durable there.
- Every `themed()` light value and its reason live beside the code (`web/index.html`) and in the `docs/UI.md` phase 3 table.
