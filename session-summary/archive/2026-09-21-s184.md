## 3. Technical Learnings

- ⚠️ **CHECK `DECISIONS.md` BEFORE PROPOSING, NOT AFTER — AND SAY SO WHEN
  PRESENTING A MEASUREMENT.** The `fmtFar` 37 were adjudicated the day before.
  Peter approved a floor he had already rejected, because my framing presented
  the count as new. ⚠️ **The harm is not the wasted work — it is that an
  approval obtained against an incomplete framing looks identical to a real
  one in the record.** `CLAUDE.md` already says to check the log before
  reopening anything settled; the missing half is that **a confident
  measurement is itself a framing**. Same family as
  `check-decisions-before-mocking-up`. `COPY_DECISIONS.md` **S9** exists so the
  next re-proposal meets the reason before Peter does.
- ⚠️ **A SAME-LENGTH `sed -i` WITHIN ONE SECOND LEAVES A STALE `.pyc` THAT
  PYTHON KEEPS USING.** Invalidation is (mtime **to the second**, size);
  `"dev/build/full"` and `"dev-build-full"` are both 14 bytes. After restoring
  the source, the module still imported the **mutant** — 8 tests failed against
  a file `grep` showed as correct. ⚠️ **The restore step is the one that looks
  green and isn't.** Clear `__pycache__` between every mutate/restore step and
  **print the value the interpreter actually loaded**. The roads falsification
  used `1.0` → `0.0` (also same-length) and was **re-run clean** to confirm it
  held. New memory: `same-length-sed-leaves-stale-pyc`.
- ⚠️ **A CLEANUP APPLIED TO THE DISPLAY HALF AND NOT THE METRIC HALF IS THE
  SHAPE TO LOOK FOR.** `load_roads` had a sliver floor that was
  *display-only*; the arithmetic half went unfloored for months and fed a
  published figure. `load_bike` had it the right way round. **When two sibling
  modules handle the same artefact differently, one of them is wrong.**
- **DELETING A CRUMB AND FLOORING A REAL VALUE ARE OPPOSITE ERRORS, AND THE
  RENDERING SURFACE CANNOT TELL THEM APART.** Of the three road hoods
  rendering `0.0`, two were artefacts (delete upstream) and one was a real
  26.5 m stub (floor the display). A single rule would have been wrong on one
  of them — the same per-surface lesson S7 learned, one layer down.
- ⚠️ **A GUARD THAT READS THE CONSTANT UNDER TEST MOVES WITH IT.** Every
  assertion in `test_build_site.py` reads `FULL_DIR`, so all of them would
  follow a rename silently — the `SQ_M_PER_ACRE` trap from the 2026-09-07
  vacuous-guard audit (784 tests green while a published figure doubled).
  `test_full_dir_is_pinned_to_its_literal` writes the string out.
- ⚠️ **I HIT THE `|`-IN-A-TABLE-CELL TRAP AGAIN**, having read that exact
  warning earlier in the same session (`|| echo` inside the cell). Then
  **misdiagnosed the second failure as the same thing** — it was that
  `check_decisions_log.py` reads **`cells[1]`, the BODY column**, and my test
  names were only in the citations column. **Read the guard the second time.**
- **VERIFY A WORKFLOW BY RUNNING ITS REAL BODY, NOT A RETYPED COPY.** Pulling
  `run:` out of the parsed YAML and executing it against throwaway bare repos
  tested the shipped text. A YAML shape test cannot do this; it can only stop
  the thing being deleted.
