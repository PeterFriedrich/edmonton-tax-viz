## 3. Technical Learnings

### 3a. ⚠️ THE BIG ONE — a published column was understating one hood by 68%

The permits CSV writes `neighbourhood` as a **comma-joined LIST** on some rows
(`OLIVER, WÎHKWÊNTÔWIN`, `THE HAMPTONS, GRANVILLE`). Those rows fell out of the
name join entirely.

**Why it hid for two months:** *every affected row predates 2021.* The 5yr window
loses **1 unit** and 3yr loses **0** — so `load_permits`' docstring called it
"immaterial" and was **right about the windows anyone had measured**. The anchored
`_long` window (published since 2026-07-21) lost **1,810 units / 1.11%**.

| | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| WÎHKWÊNTÔWIN | 1,229 | **2,066** (+68%) |
| SOUTH TERWILLEGAR | 823 | **1,189** (+45%) |
| HERITAGE VALLEY AREA | 0 | (still 0 — its 28u are tier 2) |

**Fixed:** 8 unambiguous names in `PERMIT_NAME_CORRECTIONS` = 1,245 units.
⚠️ **The invariant that proves it: citywide is UNCHANGED at 162,414.** Attribution
moved, totals did not — the check that says units were *placed*, not invented.
**565 units / 15 names stay unattributed by decision** (straddle 2+ real hoods);
a test pins that non-fix so a later tidy-up can't invent a split.

⚠️ **`a-finer-rendering-audits-the-aggregate`, third instance** — the per-year
series exposed a two-month-old defect in the aggregate on its first run.

### 3b. ⚠️ I GOT THE TIER SPLIT WRONG, AND THE ERROR WAS ONE STEP FROM SHIPPING

First pass called it 1,273/537 by checking each comma-part against boundary names

### 3e. Columns, not a line — and the choice dissolved a rule

The chart is **columns, zero-based**, deliberately not the temporal lens's line
and **not `temporalGeom`**:
1. Annual **FLOW** of discrete counts vs assessment's continuous **STOCK** — a
   line interpolates and so draws construction in years that had none. **61% of
   hood-years are exactly zero**, so that is the common case.
2. `temporalGeom` scales to the series' own min..max — a **non-zero baseline**,
   correct for share (`SPEC_temporal.md` §2 invariant 2), a **lie for counts**.

⚠️ **This retired the minimum-non-zero-year gate the feature was specced with.** A
line through one point implies a trend; one column doesn't. Only **all-zero** hoods
get words. *A decision one level up dissolved the rule instead of the rule needing
enforcement* — worth remembering as a pattern.

- ⚠️ **`syncHoodModePod`** gated the mode pod on `temporalData` alone — a deploy

### 3g. ⚠️ Probe-writing gotchas that cost ~20 minutes each

- **`window.state` is permanently undefined.** The app's top-level `let` bindings
  live in the global **lexical** environment — reachable as bare names, never as
  `window` properties. `waitForFunction(() => window.state…)` can only time out.
  The house idiom is `await page.waitForTimeout(4000)` then bare identifiers.
- **The Value metric key is `value_per_acre`**, not `assessed_value_per_acre`. A
  wrong key makes `applyMetric` a silent no-op, leaving Money on its revenue cut —
  which then looks like a regression in the panel dispatch. **Three of my probe's
  first failures were probe bugs, not app bugs.**
- Probes need the SwiftShader launch args (`--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader
  --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`) or deck throws on this GPU-less box.
- ⚠️ **A throwaway probe caught copy review missed**: `"3,806 new permits
  permitted"` from a shared noun table.

### 3h. ⚠️ My own `restart()` helper self-killed a background job (exit 144)

A loop doing `pid=$(ss -ltnp | grep 8947 …); kill $pid` inside a backgrounded
compound command died with **exit 144**, shells alive with no `node` children —
the `pgrep/pkill match themselves` memory, one tool over. **Run verify scripts one
at a time in the foreground with an explicit `timeout`**, and grep `':8947'` (with
the colon) rather than the bare port.
