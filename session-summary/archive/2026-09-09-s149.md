## 3. Technical Learnings

### THE FINDING — the operating rate is $/LANE-km applied to CENTRELINE metres

**This is the session's one reader-moving result and the reason to read on.**
Both halves of `roadway_ops` ($5,970 maintenance + $3,350 snow per km) divide by
the City's *"~11,000 km"*. That figure is the City's **snow-and-ice inventory,
stated in LANE-km**, and it includes ~1,300 km of alleys. The pipeline then
multiplies the resulting rate by **centreline** metres.

Measured from `data/raw/roads.geojson` in EPSG:3400 (the same feed `load_roads`
reads):

| population | centreline km |
|---|---|
| everything (roads + alleys + railways, all owners) | 7,700 |
| `centerline_type == Road`, all owners | 5,685 (Province 495 = the ring road) |
| **City of Edmonton roads** | **5,029** — arterial 1,358 · collector 926 · local 2,740 |
| City alleys | **1,311** |

**There is no reading under which 11,000 is linear km** — City roads plus alleys
are 6,340. ⚠️ **The clincher is the alley cross-check**: the City reported alleys
adding *"1,250 to 1,300 kilometres"* to its snow inventory in 2021 (Taproot
2022-05-31), and alleys are one lane, so lane-km = centreline km there — and the
feed holds **1,311 alley centreline km**. That pins the unit from the City's own
two statements without needing the secondary class figures at all.

**Direction and size: the shipped $9.32/m understates by 1.8–2.2× per centreline
metre** (×1.80 on the 2020 class lane-km ÷ our centreline; ×2.19 on the feed's
5,029 km alone).

### THE DECISION — state it, do not convert (Peter, `DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-08)

Peter chose the floor. **Both conversions were declined**, and the reason is the
standing rule: ×1.80 rests on a secondary 2020 relay whose blog source is now
404, ×2.19 charges alleys nothing, and **every rate this project ships came from
a named City publication after a scoped question — never from an audit's
arithmetic.** The brief itself forbids proposing a replacement rate, for exactly
this reason.

⚠️ **What the call BOUGHT, and this is the part worth carrying forward: `floor`
stopped being a judgement and became ARITHMETIC under a stated assumption.**
Two errors of known direction now bracket the rate — the lane-km denominator
understates ~1.8–2.2×, the arterial blend overstates by `1/(0.65 + 0.35k)` with
arterials at 35% of road lane-km. Net `1.80/(0.65+0.35k) ≥ 1.06` for **k ≤ 3**,
flipping only at **k > 3.3**. Procedure C409K establishes k > 1 (arterials to
bare pavement fastest, residential to a 5 cm snowpack in 10–14 days); **no k is
published anywhere.** So the honest statement is *"a floor unless arterials cost
more than ~3.3× a collector/local lane-km"* — never an unconditional bound.

⚠️ **The 5.4× lifecycle-vs-operating gap is INFLATED by this mismatch and must
not be read as a fact about a road metre.** The lifecycle $600k/$1.9M are per km
of **one** neighbourhood road — i.e. centreline — so only the operating side
carries the error. Per centreline metre the real gap is **~2.5–3×**. This is why
L2b was scoped UNSOUND for the *operating basis only*.

⚠️ **The 5× is stated in THREE places** (layer blurb, Data & Methods pod, hood
panel note) and all three were edited together. Fixing one and leaving two is
the exact split treatment that let $1,285 ship for a month while `DATA.md` §16
had already retired it.

### What the audit DISPROVED — two of the brief's own claims were ours, not the world's

- **The $182.7M vs $180.4M near-match is a coincidence, decomposed.** +$43.8M of
  O&M on our side (operating money, never funded from the renewal levy) and
  ~+$90M of non-road components on the City's (alleys, sidewalks, lighting —
  **53%** of a typical project; Roads is **47%** per the City's own CEA-2026
  slide). They do not cancel; they land near each other.
- ⚠️ **The "1.7× disagreement between two City publications" was OUR error, and
  it is the most reusable lesson here: the Neighbourhood Renewal Programme is a
  `fund`, NOT a `service`.** `fund == "Neighborhood Renewal Reserve"` is $906.7M
  FY2023–29 — $668.2M under `Neighbourhoods` **and $238.4M under `Roads`**
  (Pleasantview, Killarney, 132 Ave, 86 St, 95 Ave, 97 St, the Minor Renewal
  Program). Filtering on `service` is right for *per-neighbourhood
  reconstruction spend* (what S136 wanted) and wrong for *what the programme
  spends*. **And divide by the four-year cycle, never the FY span** — $716.5M ÷
  7 gave the phantom "$102M/yr". Correctly: **2023–26 draws $720.0M = $180.0M/yr
  vs the levy's flat $174.386M/yr, within 3%** — and ⚠️ **that agreement is an
  accounting identity, not a corroboration** (same portal, inflow vs outflow).

### Method notes that generalise

- ⚠️ **Match on the value, not a substring.** The first pass over
  `capital_budget.csv` grepped `fund` for `enewal|evy` and swept in

  was written.

  use `pkill -f` on a pattern your own command contains.
- **A test can pin shipped prose verbatim**, and one did:
  `test_the_real_prose_keeps_the_copy_and_drops_the_comments` asserts
  `"$5,970 per kilometre to maintain" in prose(html)` as its *sample* of

  updated to the shipped string. **That is the assertion working, not breaking.**
- **`check_cost_copy.py` needed no change** — it matches money strings and
  DERIVES the ratio word from the values (`_times_in_words(50/9.32)` → "five
  times"). Since no value moved, "five times" stayed valid and the guard stayed
