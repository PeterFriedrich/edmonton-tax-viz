## 3. Technical Learnings

### A retracted premise does not retract the conclusion — and nothing tracks that
The change lens stayed flat for six days on a reason known false since
2026-08-11. The comment recorded the retraction *and kept the conclusion*, with
a "do not re-open this" warning on top. **When a decision's stated reason falls,
something must re-examine the decision** — the retraction note is not enough.

### A guard going red is evidence about the DATA, not just about the guard
Six checks broke on hardcoded years. The reflex is to update the constants. Both
scripts were **correct**; the data had moved under them, and one of them was
reporting a year-old defect nobody knew about.

### Reproduce against the UNMODIFIED file before claiming a failure is pre-existing
`git show HEAD:web/index.html > /tmp/base-web/index.html`, symlink the rest of
`web/`, serve on a second port. Cheap, and it is what turned "my change broke
six checks" into "these six checks are telling us something."

### The archive was the *fix* for a trap, and became the trap
`SPEC_temporal.md` §0.4 built `write_archive` to stop 2025 being lost when the
roll advanced. It anticipated **losing** a year, never **capturing the wrong
one**, and its freeze rule now preserves the error. **A safety mechanism whose
input is unverified inherits the defect and makes it permanent.**

### Verify what you publish as fact, even when you wrote it yesterday
The `/notebooks/` index states invariant counts. Re-derived them from the
rendered HTML (6 and 11 distinct claims) rather than quoting the S120 handoff.
They were right — but the page asserts them.

### Check the specific claim, not the family
Nearly filed "Edmonton publishes no road service life". **Roads have one**; it
is *bikeways and shared pathways* that lack it. One `sed` on `DATA.md:1136`
caught it.

### Environment quirks (this box)
- ⚠️ **System `python` is 3.6.8** — no `http.server --directory`. Use
  `.venv/bin/python`, or `cd` in the *same* command.
- ⚠️ **`cd` in a compound `Bash` call does not persist** between calls.
- ⚠️ **Playwright lives in `tools/profiling/node_modules`** — scripts must run
  from there; `/tmp/foo.js` cannot `require('playwright')`.
- ⚠️ **Almost no fonts** (`Cantarell`, `DejaVu Sans Mono`, `Fixed`). Force
  Cantarell via `addStyleTag` or HTML screenshots read as monospace.
- ⚠️ **Socrata `q7d6-ambg` types EVERY numeric column as `text`** — unguarded
  `sum()` returns **HTTP 400**. Use `::number`.
- ⚠️ **`open.alberta.ca` 403s urllib's default User-Agent.**
- HTTPS `000` from this box is the **CA bundle**, not the host — use `certifi`.
