  PDFs and edmonton.ca annual-report PDFs fetch fine. WebFetch mangles binary
  PDFs and strips links from HTML pages — curl + pdftotext / grep is the
  reliable path.

  application.pdf` on epcor.com is EPCOR Water **(West)** Inc. — French Creek,
