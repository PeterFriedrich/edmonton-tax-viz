## 3. Technical Learnings

- ⚠️ **MY INSTRUMENT MANUFACTURED A FALSE HEADLINE, TWICE, AND BOTH TIMES IT
  FLATTERED THE WORK.**
  1. **Cold-load run 1 reported the 8 MB `value_grid_50.json` as a boot fetch** —
     a dramatic "the lazy architecture is broken" finding. It was Playwright's
     `response.body()` **materializing a body for the app's 394-byte HEAD probe**.
     `index.html`'s own comment ("the fine grid is never prefetched") was right
     and the measurement was wrong. Caught by re-running with **HTTP methods
     recorded**.
  2. **The mobile sweep printed a confident ceiling from "+ peek" states in which
     no peek card ever opened.** The tap hit the unfolded Options panel; the fix
     that tapped a *chrome-free* pixel still missed, because that pixel was **off
     the city polygon**. Two clean-looking runs. Caught only because `peek` was
     absent from the rect id list. Fixed by scanning candidates centre-out and
     **asserting `#peek` is visible before recording a row**.
  **The general shape: when an instrument and a code comment disagree, suspect the
  instrument first.** Both errors produced *more interesting* results than the
  truth, which is the tell.
- ⚠️ **ALL THREE ITEMS I PICKED HAD A WRONG OR STALE PREMISE.** Cold-load claimed
  "every payload number is on-disk" (false since 2026-08-10 — `PERFORMANCE.md`
  had wire figures all along). Mobile predicted Services returning public would
  carry its 53.1% (false — public Services is roads-only, 38.8%). DATA VINTAGE
  carried two dates two months stale. **`todo-can-lag-executed-work` is not an
  occasional hazard here; it was 3 for 3.** Re-check the premise *before*
  measuring, not after.
- ⚠️ **`MOBILE_USABILITY.md`'s 52.3% was WRONG WHEN WRITTEN, not drifted.** The
  worst public state is **Money unfolded + peek**; the table lists Money unfolded
  (47.9%) and default+peek (34.5%) as separate rows and **never combines them**,
  and all three affordances were public on 2026-08-04 too. The table's own rule
  ("name the state AND name the view") failed on the table itself.
- ⚠️ **`Development UNFOLDED 52.7%` is internally impossible** — a union cannot
  shrink when a rect is added, yet the next row has Development unfolded **+
  peek** at 52.3%. Re-measured 44.7% / 52.3%. **An internal contradiction is a
  cheaper staleness detector than a re-measurement** — it needs no tooling.
- **Public build ≠ full build, and the gap is large.** Public `#views` is Money ·
  Development · Services · Ratio, verified **by visibility, not DOM presence**
  (Uses and Lab are in the markup on both builds). Full is 66.1% at worst vs
  public 53.5%, but the two are **not comparable** — `#budget-pod` is full-only
  and did not exist in August.
- **The app shell is a third payload budget nobody was accounting for.**
  `index.html` is **147 KB gzipped — 15.5% of the critical path and 77% of the
  boot GeoJSON** it is never compared against, and it grows with every lens.
- **Pages serves gzip ONLY — no brotli, no zstd** (probed per-encoding, confirmed
  with a browser sending all four). ~15–20% more off the JS exists; GitHub's
  lever, not ours.
- **The idle prefetch is 52% of the whole cold visit** (1,124 KB). Deliberate
  (2026-09-01), `Save-Data`-gated — but "libraries + boot geometry" as a
  cold-load budget **undercounts the real visit by 2.3×**.
- **`VIZ_STACK.md` and `PERFORMANCE.md` contradicted each other for five weeks**
  on whether `temporal.json` / `reference.geojson` / `dev_history.json` are
  toggle-driven. They are **unconditional boot fetches** (three adjacent lines in
  `index.html`); `PERFORMANCE.md` was right. `dev_history.json` was boot cost in
