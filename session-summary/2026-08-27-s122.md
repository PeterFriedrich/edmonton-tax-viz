## 3. Technical Learnings

### A safety mechanism whose input is unverified consumes its one chance
The archive was built to stop 2025 being lost. It ran on schedule and captured
the wrong year, because the guard blessing its input read *metadata* rather than
measuring *data*. **It did not merely fail — it spent the single opportunity it
existed to protect, and the freeze rule then made that permanent.**

### Measure the consequence of a fix BEFORE applying it
"Delete the bad entry and 2025 comes back" was wrong, and one `publishable_years`
call before the delete caught it. Had I deleted first and looked after, the
session would have shipped a silent year-loss as a fix.

### Presence is not correctness — and one check cannot answer both
`check_temporal_archive` (was it captured?) was green all along. The question it
does **not** ask is whether what was captured is what it claims to be.

### A duplicated comparison is a bypassed decision
The stale-metadata downgrade lived inside `check_alignment()`. A second caller
reimplemented the comparison and never got it — green for two days, and would
have cried wolf monthly. **Route through the function that owns the decision.**

### A guard reading a publisher's free-text field is not measuring the data
Third time this exact shape has bitten (levy $69.5M, the archive, the digest).
**All three were found by accident, not by looking** — see Next Steps 3.

### Skipped tests read as green
Three new tests silently skipped because my FIR fixture guessed the wrong
schema. `745 passed, 3 skipped` looks fine at a glance. Check the skip count.

### Environment quirks (this box) — all still true
- ⚠️ **Playwright lives in `tools/profiling/node_modules`** — a script in `/tmp`
  **cannot** `require('playwright')`. Copy it into that directory.
- ⚠️ **Run verify scripts ONE AT A TIME** — a 4-script loop timed out at 2 min
  and manufactured a phantom `nonres_revenue` failure that vanished alone.
- ⚠️ **A `Bash` heredoc editing `web/index.html` does NOT fire the CODEMAP
  hook** (only `Edit`/`Write` do) — run `tools/codemap.py` by hand after.
- ⚠️ System `python` is 3.6.8 — use `.venv/bin/python`. HTTPS `000` is the CA
  bundle; use `certifi`.
- ⚠️ `_site/` is build output, **not gitignored and never committed** — delete
  it after running `build_site.py`, don't stage it.
