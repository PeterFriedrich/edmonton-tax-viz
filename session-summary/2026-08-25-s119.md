## 3. Technical Learnings

### A guard that reads a publisher's metadata field is not measuring the data
The whole S119 chain hangs off this. `Period of Coverage` is hand-maintained by
Edmonton; it went stale, and **every guard in the project agreed with it.** The
replacement finds a quantity that should match an external source almost
exactly (residential taxable assessment, barely exempt anywhere) and uses its
**residual** as the detector — +1.2% for the right year, ~10% for a neighbour.

### Re-measuring a stale blocker means re-deriving its CAUSE, not its SYMPTOM
The `000` was real and reproduced. The *cause* was never checked, and the wrong
cause made a one-line client fix look like a hardware constraint for 13 days.
Decompose: `getent hosts` → `curl -v` → `openssl s_client -showcerts`.

### One mapping cannot answer two questions
`ZONE_CATEGORY` answers *"can this be developed?"* (`PS` → `never`: **correct**,
parks aren't infill) and was also being used for *"might this be exempt?"*
(`PS` → `never`: **wrong**, parks are prime candidates). ⚠️ **Moving `PS` into
`inst` would repair the band and break the development lens.** Two sets, each
commented with why the other exists.

### A mislabelled column hides a defect indefinitely
`inst_frac` that includes parks is a lie. The rename to `exempt_*` was not
cosmetic — the wrong name is what made "the band excludes parks" invisible.

### ⚠️ Silence is not success in the verify scripts
`verify-deviation.js` reported **0 passes, 0 failures** and I nearly took it as
a pass. It hardcodes **port 8777** and `?build=full`, and had failed to connect.
Always check the PASS count, never just the absence of FAIL.
