## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ A CLEARED HOST IS NOT A FULLY PROBED HOST
`budget.edmonton.ca` was proven reachable on 2026-08-13 and the finding was
recorded — but only for `operating_budget.csv`. **The capital budget sat one
`curl` away for nine days behind a host we had already declared open.** The
2026-08-13 lesson was *"test the specific host before recording a blocker"*;
its sibling is *"a host that answered one endpoint has other endpoints"*.
Sister to `todo-can-lag-executed-work` and S113's *"blocked on data" was a claim*.

### ⚠️ `df.head()` IS A VERIFICATION STEP, NOT A COURTESY
Row counts, sums and `groupby` summaries all looked fine and were all
*compatible with three defects*. Ten raw rows exposed the negative reallocations
(visible only as an adjacent ± pair), the repeated-profile grain, and the fact
that my stated join warning had compared grains rather than populations.
**Look at the rows, the same way S113 learned to look at the render.**

### ⚠️ THE ABSENCE OF A FRESHNESS HEADER IS A DESIGN INPUT, NOT A NUISANCE
Every other check in `vintage_report.py` keys off Socrata's `rowsUpdatedAt`.
This endpoint has none (`Last-Modified` echoes `Date`), which is precisely what
forced *the committed file to be the pin* — and therefore fused "commit the
data" and "add a notification" into a single change. **Check for a date field
before designing a staleness check; its absence changes the shape of the answer.**

### ⚠️ HASH SORTED ROWS WHEN THE PAYLOAD IS GENERATED PER REQUEST
Byte-stability measured today does not survive a server-side regeneration.
A raw-bytes hash would have gone ACTION on a reorder that changed no figure.

### AN EXTERNAL FIGURE CAN AGREE WITH YOURS AND STILL NOT BE A SOURCE
The external numbers matched ours *to the dollar* — because both derive from the
same City page. ⚠️ **That is a tautology, not corroboration**, exactly as
`DATA.md` §18 says of `da9s-v9j8` vs the portal CSV. Agreement between two
things with one origin is worth zero confidence.
