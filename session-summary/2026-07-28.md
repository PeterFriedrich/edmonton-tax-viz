**CARDINALITY VERIFICATION (doc-first, then live — both bugs are NON-ISSUES):**
- **The neighbourhood lens is immune BY CONSTRUCTION**, not by a fix: numerator
  is `groupby(neighbourhood).sum()` over accounts and **never joins to parcel
  geometry**; denominator is boundary-polygon area and **never reads
  `lot_size`**. Two different bases. Both bugs can only distort *per-parcel /
  per-cell* metrics.
- **Bug 1 (WEM) premise is INVERTED.** WEM is a **single $1,285.1M account on
  one point** in Summerlea, not multiple records on one parcel. Citywide only
  **1.0% of points carry >1 account** (2,996 of 287,123). The real WEM artifact
  is a *grid needle*, addressed by the lot-acre denominator: **$12.6M/ground-acre
  → $290k/lot-acre**, and the top tower ($612.3M/lot-acre) beats it ~50×.
- **Bug 2 (condos) is largely false in this dataset** — `lot_size` at condo
  points is mostly null or already apportioned, not duplicated. Citywide
  overcount **0.1%**; worst hood +12% (River Valley Walterdale).
- **`tools/audit_cardinality_denominators.py` re-run reproduced EVERY documented
  figure exactly** — but against the **Jul 6 snapshot** in `data/raw/`, which is
  what the audit was written against. It is a reproducibility check, not a
  freshness check. That limitation is exactly why the guard exists.
- **No regression test pins WEM or a named condo** and there still isn't one by
  name — the guard pins the *regime* instead, deliberately.

**THE BANNER QUESTION (Peter: "should this tie into the existing 'last year's
data' fail type?") — it already does, and the ORDERING is load-bearing.**
- The guard sits at workflow step **108**, *before* "Update status manifest"
  (112) and the commit (122). On drift: job fails → `last_checked` NOT bumped →
  nothing committed → site serves last-good data → the frontend's own
  `stalenessBanner()` fires at `STALE_DAYS = 14`.
- **If the guard ran after the status step the heartbeat would bump and the
  failure would be invisible.** Same reason `check_unmatched_names` sits before
  regen.
- **Drift is deliberately NOT the year-hold fail type.** Year-hold is *known,
  benign, expected* (new assessment year upstream; data correct, just last
  year's) → job stays GREEN with a specific reassuring banner. Drift is
  *unknown* → hard-fail, because a green job with a friendly banner reads as
  routine when it isn't. Viewer-facing they converge; owner-facing they must
  differ.
- **No per-guard banner was added on purpose.** Every refresh failure already
  tells one viewer story through the heartbeat. `STALE_DAYS = 14` against a
  **weekly cron** (Mon 08:00 UTC) = one missed run tolerated, two consecutive
  misses warn — a chosen number, not arbitrary. If too slow for a public
  audience, that is a **global** knob.
