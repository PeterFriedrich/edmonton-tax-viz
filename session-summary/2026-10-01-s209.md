## 3. Technical Learnings
- **A vacuous check, caught by falsifying.** The first no-clipboard check opened `#view=services&on=none` and expected that hash in the bar. With the hash-strip ALSO mutated, the restored hash satisfied the check while the fallback was deleted.
  - Fix: reach the state by a click on a clean page.
  - The `check-where-the-value-can-be-wrong` lesson again: the fixture must differ only on the condition under test.
- **Headless Chromium's clipboard works** with `permissions: ['clipboard-read', 'clipboard-write']` on `localhost` over HTTP. localhost counts as a secure context, and CI serves the site there too.
- The bottom-right pods are 28px tall with borders, not the 26px button height, and they sit flush with no gap. The "2px gap" in the old comments is really the border.
