## 3. Technical Learnings

**⚠️ THE HYPOTHESIS IN A TICKET IS NOT EVIDENCE EITHER — AND APPLYING IT IS A
CHEAP WAY TO FIND OUT.** The standing rule was *reproduce the symptom before
fixing*. The Display bug extends it. Its `TODO.md` entry had carried a suspected
cause for six sessions (`#about.open` lifts to `z-index: 5`; `#a11y` has no
equivalent). Both the **direction** and the **fix** were wrong:
- **Direction:** equal `z-index` falls back to DOM order and `#about` is later,
  so the **button paints over the MENU**, truncating *"Landmarks & nearby
  pla⌷es"* — the opposite of the report. **Found in a screenshot**;
  `elementFromPoint` then agreed.
- **Cause:** the two pods are a **stack** in one column (`bottom: 40px` / `68px`,
  both buttons 26px) and `#a11y-menu` was anchored to its **own** button's top,
  ignoring the sibling above it. Fixed offsets → the ~23px collision was
  **identical at 1440×900, 390×844 and 360×780**.
- ⚠️ **THE SUSPECTED FIX WOULD HAVE BEEN WORSE THAN THE BUG.** Applied,
  `#a11y.open { z-index: 5 }` makes `verify-about.js` **TIME OUT**: the
  *"Landmarks & nearby places"* label **intercepts pointer events** and the
  Data & Methods button becomes **UNCLICKABLE**. A visual defect for a dead
  control.
- ⚠️ **It surfaced only because `verify-about.js` drives a REAL `page.click()`.**
  A JS `.click()` bypasses `pointer-events` and would have passed — the standing
  warning about the other scripts, paid out.
- ⚠️ **So the new checks assert GEOMETRY (no overlap), NOT paint order.** "The
  menu is on top" passes for the z-index version, which is the outcome to
  reject. **When two fixes exist and one is bad, the assertion must reject
  both.**

**⚠️ FALSIFY EVERY LOAD-BEARING CHECK — three did their job this session.**
Reverting `#temporal-chart .mixbar` turns the bar `block`; removing
`syncPinnedPanel` fails the lens swap; reverting the menu anchor fails 3 of the
new geometry checks. A check never seen to fail is not known to bite.

**⚠️ A BORROWED BODY NEEDS ITS BORROWED CSS — SECOND OCCURRENCE.** `.mixbar` was
scoped `.tip .mixbar, #peek-read .mixbar`; reusing the markup in `#temporal-chart`
without adding the selector loses `display:flex` and **collapses the bar to
nothing visible**. No id-based assertion can see it. Same shape as S83's.

**⚠️ A FORMATTER IS CALIBRATED FOR A RANGE, AND REUSE ACROSS RANGES IS A SILENT
PRECISION BUG.** `fmtBig` (assessment totals, $10M–$10B) printed a $1,876,137
levy as **"$2M"**. `fmtMix`'s one decimal turned a 0.069% city share into
"0.1%". Both were **found by reading rendered output**, not by a test — and the
same pass produced the `<0.1%` floor so a real category never prints "0.0%".

**⚠️ VERIFY-PEEK CANNOT SHARE THE BOX.** It reported **12 failures**, then
**4**, then **27/27 all green** — the difference was concurrency. Two instances
driving real pointers at one server, or a 12-script batch alongside it, and its
picking dance fails wholesale. ⚠️ **`verify-about`, `verify-center2d` and
`verify-reference-layer` flaked the same way in batches and were green in
isolation and on clean master.** **Run real-pointer scripts alone.**

**⚠️ `pgrep -f "<pattern>"` IN A WAIT LOOP MATCHES ITS OWN COMMAND LINE** and
never exits. Cost several dead waiters (and four stale ones were still running
from an earlier session). Use a bracket: `pgrep -f "verify-peek[.]js"`.

**`#toggle`'s two row-2s are DIFFERENT KINDS OF NESTING**, and the comment now
says so: `#revcut`'s cuts are genuine **subsets** (`levy == res + nonres +
farmland`), while `#moneymode` is two **lenses** on one quantity. Both render as
subordinate, but a future reader must not infer that everything in row 2 sums to
its parent.

**ONE CATEGORY SET, ENFORCED BY DERIVATION.** The revenue panel derives its
column as `"rev_" + u.frac` over `USE_CATEGORIES` rather than listing the
categories again — `revenue_by_zone.py`'s `OUTPUT_NAMES` mirror those keys
deliberately. The Uses lens's **area** shares and this panel's **revenue** shares
cannot drift onto two vocabularies. `ARCHITECTURE.md` records the coupling.
