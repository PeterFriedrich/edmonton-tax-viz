**⚠️ THE HYPOTHESIS IN A TICKET IS NOT EVIDENCE EITHER — AND APPLYING IT IS A
CHEAP WAY TO FIND OUT.** The standing rule was *reproduce the symptom before
fixing*. The Display bug extends it. Its `TODO.md` entry had carried a suspected
cause for six sessions (`#about.open` lifts to `z-index: 5`; `#a11y` has no

- **Direction:** equal `z-index` falls back to DOM order and `#about` is later,
  so the **button paints over the MENU**, truncating *"Landmarks & nearby
  pla⌷es"* — the opposite of the report. **Found in a screenshot**;
  `elementFromPoint` then agreed.
- **Cause:** the two pods are a **stack** in one column (`bottom: 40px` / `68px`,

  ignoring the sibling above it. Fixed offsets → the ~23px collision was
  **identical at 1440×900, 390×844 and 360×780**.
- ⚠️ **THE SUSPECTED FIX WOULD HAVE BEEN WORSE THAN THE BUG.** Applied,
  `#a11y.open { z-index: 5 }` makes `verify-about.js` **TIME OUT**: the
  *"Landmarks & nearby places"* label **intercepts pointer events** and the
  Data & Methods button becomes **UNCLICKABLE**. A visual defect for a dead
  control.
- ⚠️ **It surfaced only because `verify-about.js` drives a REAL `page.click()`.**
  A JS `.click()` bypasses `pointer-events` and would have passed — the standing
  warning about the other scripts, paid out.
- ⚠️ **So the new checks assert GEOMETRY (no overlap), NOT paint order.** "The
  menu is on top" passes for the z-index version, which is the outcome to
  reject. **When two fixes exist and one is bad, the assertion must reject

## 3. Technical Learnings

**⚠️ A CHECK CAN BE HALF OF THE BUG — BOTH ITEMS THIS SESSION WERE THAT SHAPE.**
Neither check asserted the wrong thing; both had the wrong **coverage**.
`panel clears the title` was green through five failures because it ran only on
money/value — *the same narrow state the 210px constant was measured from*. The
literal pins were green until data moved because they had never seen a refresh.
**When a check and the thing it guards are derived from the same narrow
observation, the check cannot fail for the reason it exists.**

**⚠️ A FRONT-END CHECK THAT CONTRADICTS A PIPELINE GUARD IS THE ONE THAT IS
WRONG.** `check_temporal_years.py` refuses to band the live year *by documented
decision*. `verify-temporal.js` pinned it to equalities. Before re-pinning any
literal, check whether something upstream already ruled on whether that quantity
is pinnable.

**⚠️ DERIVE > BAND, when the guard says a value legitimately moves.** A band
still needs a width guess and still drifts. Deriving from the loaded series
cannot cry wolf **and** additionally catches the wrong hood / series / index.
⚠️ **But compare PARSED NUMBERS** — building the expectation with the page's own
formatter makes formatter bugs invisible (S85 shipped one).

**⚠️ `max-height` IS CONTENT-BOX BY DEFAULT.** A JS-computed max-height means
*content*, not the box you can see — here 21px of padding + border, so the panel
overshot by 11px in states that did not even scroll. `box-sizing: border-box` on
the element makes the computation mean what it says.

**⚠️ AN ABSOLUTELY-POSITIONED CHILD OF A SCROLL CONTAINER SCROLLS AWAY.** Adding
`overflow-y: auto` to a panel took its close button and hood name out of view.
Anything that must survive a scroll belongs OUTSIDE the scrolling region — hence
the `#temporal-body` wrapper.

**⚠️ THE DEV SERVER SERVES THE FULL BUILD, SO PUBLIC-BUILD BREAKAGE IS INVISIBLE
LOCALLY.** #141 exists because #140 was green on `localhost:8931` and red on the
public root. **Run new control-driving checks against the deployed public URL,
