## 3. Technical Learnings

### ⚠️ THE SESSION'S THEME — a fabricated data point manufactured a trend, and the trend then loosened a guard

The open TODO item *"WHY IS VALUE LEAVING THE LOT-ACRE DENOMINATOR?"*
(2026-08-05) rested on `ineligible_points` 56 → 58 → 60 and
`ineligible_value_frac` 0.00517 → 0.00575 → 0.00633, *"monotonically upward on
every independent data change, no reversal"*. It was carried as
**the most likely thing to fail next** through the S133 and S134 handoffs.

**Three independent findings killed it:**

1. ⚠️ **THE MIDDLE OBSERVATION WAS NEVER OBSERVED.** The `58 / 0.00575` row
   matched **no CI run**. All five of its anchors were midpoints of the rows
   either side — `lot_needle_ratio` **12.82175**, exactly between 12.822 and
   12.8215; points 58, exactly between 56 and 60. It had been a pinned row in
   `OBSERVED_IN_CI` (`tests/test_check_value_anchors.py`) since 2026-08-05.
   **It is what turned a single step into an apparent monotone three-point
   trend.** The 08-01→08-05 window holds **six** runs and only **two** distinct
   data states; the step fell between the **05:17 and 11:19 runs of 08-03**,
   which the original harvest collapsed into one "08-03".
2. ⚠️ **IT REVERTED ON 08-10** and has moved ~0.3% across the 8 independent
   pulls since. `ineligible_value_frac` sits at **49.4% of its band** — dead
   centre — not the 72% its own note estimated nor the **83%** two handoffs
   carried.
3. ⚠️ **THE 83%/85 CAME FROM STALE, MISMATCHED LOCAL RAW.** This box holds
   `Property_Info__Current_Calendar_Year_.csv` dated **2026-07-06** beside a
   **2026-08-09** assessment file. Against that pair the guard reads
   `ineligible_points` **85** / `0.00646`; CI, same code, same day, fresh pull,
   read **58** / `0.00515519`.

⚠️ **AND THAT PHANTOM HAD ALREADY COST SOMETHING.** The 2026-09-02 re-pin note
claimed the reading *"had reached the band ceiling EXACTLY and would have fired
on the next weekly publish on its own"* and widened `ineligible_points`
**28–84 → 42.5–127.5**. It would not have fired — CI read 58, comfortably
inside 28–84. The ceiling moved **84 → 127.5, halving sensitivity in the
guard's own declared dangerous direction to accommodate a number the published
pipeline never produced.** (The floor move was harmless: below-band on a
high-danger anchor is `benign`, warn-only, never reds the publish.)

**Transferable rules:**
- ⚠️ **A trend needs three REAL observations — check that the middle one is
  one.** An interpolated point is indistinguishable from data once it is in a
  table, and it manufactures exactly the monotonicity that makes a trend look
  real.
- ⚠️ **A guard run on stale input is not a weaker reading, it is a DIFFERENT
  MEASUREMENT.**
- ⚠️ **Pinning a band's width without pinning its centre guards the wrong
  half.** Widths had a test since 2026-08-05; provenance had nothing.

Same family as *a band fitted around a defect* and *an assertion placed where
the value can't be wrong* (both S133), and *the 3% cross-check built from its
own inputs* (S134). **That is four consecutive sessions finding the same shape.**

### ✅ What "ineligible" actually means (nobody had written this down plainly)

A map point is many stacked records (condo units at one address). If the
**majority** of them have null or zero `lot_size`, the point has no trustworthy
parcel area, so it leaves the **lot-acre** metric **numerator AND denominator**
while staying in **ground-acre**. Currently **58 points, 4,313 records,
$1.229B assessed**. The declared danger is *growth* — the two lenses diverge
silently as the set grows. Hence hard-fail above band, warn below.
