  - Levy on exempt-candidate zoning is 8.1% of total levy.
  - It is 16.4% of non-res levy.
  - Only about 2.5% of candidate-zone levy is residential class.
- **`apply_tax_rates` already gives per-parcel `res_levy` and `nonres_levy`**, so the per-cut exempt share needs no new join.
- **Front-end consumers of `exemptFrac`:**
  - **Money:** `instShiftMoney`, `instBandedMoney`, the tooltip.
  - **Glass:** `glassInstCells`, the base height.
  - **Deviation:** reads `state.labCut`.
  - **Ratio:** stays on total, because its numerator is total revenue.
  - Mapped in the proposal.
